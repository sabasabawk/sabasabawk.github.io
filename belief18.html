Fundamental Belief #18

The Gift of Prophecy

Key Bible Texts

Joel 2:28-29

Amos 3:7

Ephesians 4:11-13

Revelation 12:17

Revelation 19:10

What We Believe

One of the gifts of the Holy Spirit is prophecy. This gift is an identifying mark of the remnant church and was manifested in the ministry of Ellen G. White.

As the Lord's messenger, her writings are a continuing and authoritative source of truth that provide comfort, guidance, instruction, and correction to the church. They make clear that the Bible is the standard by which all teaching and experience must be tested.

The gift of prophecy does not replace the Bible. Instead, it points people to Scripture and helps believers better understand and apply God's Word in their daily lives.

Why It Matters

God has always communicated with His people through prophets. The gift of prophecy strengthens faith, provides spiritual guidance, and helps prepare God's people for the challenges of the last days.

Practical Application

  • Study the Bible as the supreme authority.
  • Read inspired counsel prayerfully.
  • Apply biblical principles in daily life.
  • Seek God's guidance through prayer and Scripture.
  • Share God's truth with others.

About Ellen G. White

Ellen Gould White (1827–1915) was one of the founders of the Seventh-day Adventist Church. Through her ministry, writings, and counsel, she encouraged millions to study the Bible, follow Jesus Christ, and prepare for His soon return. Her books on Christian living, education, health, and prophecy continue to bless people around the world.

Reflection Questions

1. Why does God give the gift of prophecy to His church?

2. How can prophetic guidance strengthen my faith?

3. Am I allowing God's Word to guide my life?
