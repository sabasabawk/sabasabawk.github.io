👨‍👩‍👧 Lesson 1: God's Plan for the Family

Key Text

"And the Lord God said, It is not good that the man should be alone; I will make him an help meet for him."

Genesis 2:18

Lesson

The family was established by God in the Garden of Eden. Before there was any church, school, or government, God created the family as the foundation of society.

God created Adam and Eve and joined them together in marriage. His plan was that the home would be a place of love, unity, support, and spiritual growth.

A Christian family should reflect God's character. Family members should love, respect, forgive, encourage, and pray for one another.

Strong families do not happen by accident. They are built when family members put God first and follow biblical principles in daily life.

The Seventh-day Adventist Church teaches that healthy families are essential for healthy churches and strong communities.

God's Purpose for the Family

  • To provide love and companionship.
  • To teach children about God.
  • To support one another in life's challenges.
  • To reflect God's love to the world.
  • To prepare family members for eternal life.

Memory Verse

"As for me and my house, we will serve the Lord."

Joshua 24:15
