📖 Lesson 2: The Biblical Foundation of Education

Key Text

"All scripture is given by inspiration of God, and is profitable for doctrine, for reproof, for correction, for instruction in righteousness."

2 Timothy 3:16

Introduction

The Bible is the foundation of true education. While many subjects are valuable, God's Word provides the principles that guide every area of life. Seventh-day Adventist education is built upon Scripture because the Bible reveals God's character, His will, and His plan for humanity.

1. The Bible Is God's Inspired Word

The Scriptures were given by God to teach truth, correct error, and guide people into righteous living. Every Christian education should begin with God's Word.

2. Jesus Is the Greatest Teacher

Jesus taught with wisdom, compassion, and authority. He used stories, questions, and practical examples to help people understand God's truth.

3. Wisdom Begins with Reverence for God

Knowledge without God can lead to pride, but true wisdom begins with respecting and obeying the Lord.

"The fear of the Lord is the beginning of knowledge."

Proverbs 1:7

4. Education Should Transform Lives

Biblical education is not simply about gaining information. It should transform attitudes, strengthen faith, and prepare believers to serve others.

5. Every Subject Can Glorify God

Whether studying science, history, mathematics, language, or health, students should recognize God as the Creator and Sustainer of all truth.

Lesson Summary

  • The Bible is the foundation of true education.
  • Jesus is the perfect Teacher.
  • True wisdom begins with God.
  • Education should transform character.
  • Learning should glorify God.
