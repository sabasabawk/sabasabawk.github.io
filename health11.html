🌿 Lesson 11: Health Reform in the SDA Church

Key Text

"Beloved, I wish above all things that thou mayest prosper and be in health, even as thy soul prospereth."

3 John 1:2

Lesson

Health Reform is an important part of the mission of the Seventh-day Adventist Church. Adventists believe that God is interested in the complete well-being of His people—physical, mental, social, and spiritual.

In 1863, Ellen G. White received important counsel regarding healthful living. These principles encouraged proper nutrition, exercise, fresh air, water, rest, temperance, and trust in God.

The Adventist Church established hospitals, clinics, health centers, and educational institutions around the world to promote health and healing.

Health Reform is not merely about avoiding disease. It is about honoring God, enjoying abundant life, and being better prepared to serve Him and others.

Today, Adventists are known worldwide for promoting healthy lifestyles and preventive health practices.

Main Principles of Health Reform

  • Healthy nutrition.
  • Regular exercise.
  • Pure water.
  • Fresh air and sunshine.
  • Adequate rest.
  • Temperance and self-control.
  • Trust in God.
