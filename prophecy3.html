📖 Lesson 3: Nebuchadnezzar's Dream – The Great Image

Key Text

"And in the days of these kings shall the God of heaven set up a kingdom, which shall never be destroyed."

Daniel 2:44

Introduction

One night King Nebuchadnezzar had a troubling dream. When he awoke, he could not remember it, but he knew it was important.

God used this dream to reveal the future of the world from Babylon until the establishment of His eternal kingdom.

The King's Challenge

Nebuchadnezzar called the wise men of Babylon and demanded that they tell him both the dream and its interpretation.

The wise men could not do it. They admitted that only God could reveal such a mystery.

Daniel Seeks God

Daniel and his friends prayed earnestly for God's help.

That night God revealed both the dream and its meaning to Daniel.

Daniel gave all the glory to God.

The Great Image

The king saw a great image made of different metals:

  • Head of Gold
  • Chest and Arms of Silver
  • Belly and Thighs of Bronze
  • Legs of Iron
  • Feet of Iron and Clay

A stone cut without human hands struck the image, destroyed it, and became a great mountain that filled the whole earth.

The Meaning of the Metals

The image represented successive world empires:

  • Gold – Babylon
  • Silver – Medo-Persia
  • Bronze – Greece
  • Iron – Rome
  • Iron and Clay – Divided Nations of Europe

History has fulfilled this prophecy exactly as God predicted.

The Stone Kingdom

The stone represents Jesus Christ and His eternal kingdom.

Unlike earthly kingdoms, God's kingdom will never be destroyed.

At Christ's Second Coming, all human governments will pass away and God's kingdom will reign forever.

Lessons From the Dream

  • God knows the future.
  • Bible prophecy is trustworthy.
  • Earthly kingdoms rise and fall.
  • Jesus will establish His everlasting kingdom.

Application

The prophecy of Daniel 2 reminds us that world events are under God's control. Our hope should not be in earthly powers but in the coming kingdom of Christ.
