🎯 Lesson 4: The Purpose of True Education

Key Text

"And Jesus increased in wisdom and stature, and in favour with God and man."

Luke 2:52

Introduction

Many people believe that education exists only to prepare students for employment or financial success. While these are important, God's purpose for education is much greater. True education prepares people to know God, develop Christlike character, serve others faithfully, and become responsible citizens while preparing for eternal life.

1. To Know God

The highest purpose of education is to lead people into a personal relationship with God. Knowledge without knowing God is incomplete.

2. To Develop Christlike Character

True education shapes honesty, kindness, humility, self-control, patience, and love. Character is more valuable than academic achievement alone.

3. To Prepare for Service

God gives every person talents to bless others. Education should prepare students to use their gifts in service to God, their families, the church, and society.

4. To Develop the Whole Person

Seventh-day Adventist education encourages balanced growth in the physical, mental, spiritual, and social aspects of life.

5. To Prepare for Eternity

The final goal of Christian education is to prepare people to live forever with Jesus in His eternal kingdom.

Lesson Summary

  • True education leads us to know God.
  • It develops Christlike character.
  • It prepares us to serve others.
  • It develops the whole person.
  • It prepares us for eternal life.
