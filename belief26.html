Fundamental Belief #26

Death and Resurrection

Key Bible Texts

Ecclesiastes 9:5

John 11:11-14

1 Corinthians 15:51-54

1 Thessalonians 4:13-18

John 5:28-29

What We Believe

The wages of sin is death. Death is a state of unconsciousness for all people. When a person dies, their thoughts cease and they rest in the grave until the resurrection.

The only One who possesses immortality is God. Eternal life is given to believers through Jesus Christ. Until Christ returns, those who have died are asleep in Him.

At the Second Coming of Christ, the righteous dead will be resurrected and together with the righteous living will receive immortality and be taken to heaven with Jesus.

Hope Beyond Death

  • Death is not the end for believers.
  • Jesus conquered death through His resurrection.
  • The righteous will rise again at Christ's return.
  • God will reunite families and loved ones.
  • Eternal life is promised to all who trust in Christ.

Why It Matters

This teaching brings comfort during times of loss and grief. Christians can face death with hope because Jesus has promised a future resurrection and eternal life.

Practical Application

  • Trust Jesus as the source of eternal life.
  • Comfort others with the hope of the resurrection.
  • Live each day in readiness for Christ's return.
  • Study and share God's promises about eternal life.
  • Remain faithful even during trials and loss.

Reflection Questions

1. How does the promise of resurrection strengthen my faith?

2. What comfort do I find in Christ's victory over death?

3. How can I share this hope with others?
