SDA History Lesson 12

The Church Today and Future Mission

Introduction

Today the Seventh-day Adventist Church is a global movement with millions of members in nearly every country in the world. The church continues to focus on preaching the Gospel, preparing people for the second coming of Jesus Christ, and serving humanity.

Global Presence

The Adventist Church operates in:

  • More than 200 countries and territories
  • Thousands of churches worldwide
  • Schools, colleges, and universities
  • Hospitals and clinics
  • Media and publishing ministries

Modern Evangelism

Today’s mission work uses modern tools such as:

  • Television and radio programs
  • Social media evangelism
  • Online Bible studies
  • Mobile apps and websites
  • Global satellite evangelism

These tools help spread the Gospel faster than ever before.

Health and Education Today

The Adventist Church continues to operate one of the largest Protestant health and education systems in the world.

These institutions continue to serve communities and support mission work.

Challenges in the Modern Era

  • Secularism and unbelief
  • Rapid cultural changes
  • Economic challenges
  • Religious opposition in some areas
  • Technological distractions

Despite these challenges, the church continues to grow.

Future Mission Focus

The church’s mission for the future is based on Revelation 14:6-12: the three angels’ messages.

The focus is:

  • Preaching the everlasting Gospel
  • Calling people to worship God as Creator
  • Preparing for the second coming of Christ
  • Living holy and faithful lives

Hope for the Future

Adventists believe that Jesus Christ will soon return to end suffering, sin, and death. The church continues to prepare people for this great event.

A new earth will be created where believers will live eternally with God.

Conclusion

From a small group of believers in the 1800s, the Seventh-day Adventist Church has grown into a worldwide movement. Its mission continues until the return of Jesus Christ.
