🎁 Lesson 3: Stewardship of Talents

Key Text

"As every man hath received the gift, even so minister the same one to another, as good stewards of the manifold grace of God."

1 Peter 4:10

Introduction

God has given every person unique talents, abilities, and spiritual gifts. These gifts are not given merely for personal benefit but to glorify God and serve others.

A faithful steward recognizes that talents come from God and should be developed and used wisely.

The Parable of the Talents

In Matthew 25:14-30, Jesus told the Parable of the Talents. The servants who used and multiplied their talents were praised by their master, while the servant who hid his talent was rebuked.

This parable teaches that God expects us to use what He has entrusted to us faithfully.

Different Gifts, Same Purpose

God gives different gifts to different people:

  • Teaching
  • Preaching
  • Singing and music
  • Leadership
  • Encouragement
  • Service and helping others
  • Writing and communication
  • Administration

No gift is unimportant. Every talent can be used to honor God.

Developing Your Talents

  • Pray for God's guidance.
  • Practice and improve your skills.
  • Use your gifts regularly.
  • Serve others through your abilities.
  • Give God the glory for every success.

Stewardship and Mission

God's gifts are meant to advance His kingdom. Whether through preaching, teaching, music, health ministry, or service, our talents should help lead people to Christ.
