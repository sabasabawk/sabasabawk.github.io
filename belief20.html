Fundamental Belief #20

The Sabbath

Key Bible Texts

Genesis 2:1-3

Exodus 20:8-11

Isaiah 58:13-14

Mark 2:27-28

Luke 4:16

Hebrews 4:4-10

What We Believe

The gracious Creator, after the six days of Creation, rested on the seventh day and instituted the Sabbath for all people as a memorial of Creation. The fourth commandment of God's unchangeable law requires the observance of this seventh-day Sabbath as the day of rest, worship, and ministry.

The Sabbath is a day of delightful communion with God and one another. It is a symbol of our redemption in Christ, a sign of our sanctification, a token of our loyalty, and a foretaste of our eternal future in God's kingdom.

From sunset Friday to sunset Saturday, believers celebrate God's creative and redeeming acts through worship, fellowship, service, Bible study, and rest from ordinary labor.

Why the Sabbath Matters

  • It reminds us that God is our Creator.
  • It points to salvation through Jesus Christ.
  • It strengthens our relationship with God.
  • It provides physical, mental, and spiritual rest.
  • It unites families and believers in worship.
  • It is a sign of obedience and loyalty to God.

How Jesus Related to the Sabbath

Jesus faithfully kept the Sabbath and taught its true purpose. He declared that "The Sabbath was made for man" and used the day to worship, teach, heal, and serve others. Christ showed that the Sabbath is a blessing, not a burden.

Ways to Keep the Sabbath Holy

  1. Prepare before sunset on Friday.
  2. Attend worship services.
  3. Spend time in prayer and Bible study.
  4. Enjoy Christian fellowship.
  5. Serve others through acts of kindness.
  6. Spend time with family.
  7. Appreciate God's creation.
  8. Avoid unnecessary secular work and activities.

Reflection Questions

1. How does the Sabbath strengthen my relationship with God?

2. What can I do to make the Sabbath more meaningful?

3. How can I help others experience the blessing of the Sabbath?
