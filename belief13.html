Fundamental Belief #13

The Remnant and Its Mission

Key Bible Texts

Revelation 12:17

Revelation 14:6-12

Matthew 24:14

2 Peter 3:9

What We Believe

The universal church is composed of all who truly believe in Christ. However, in the last days, a remnant has been called out to keep the commandments of God and the faith of Jesus.

This remnant announces the arrival of the judgment hour, proclaims salvation through Christ, and prepares people for His soon return. Its mission is symbolized by the three angels' messages of Revelation 14.

The remnant church calls all people to worship the Creator, accept the righteousness of Christ, obey God's Word, and prepare for the Second Coming of Jesus Christ.

Why It Matters

This belief reminds believers that God has entrusted His church with a special mission in the last days. Every Christian is called to share the Gospel and help prepare the world for Christ's return.

Practical Application

  • Share the Gospel with others.
  • Study and understand the Three Angels' Messages.
  • Live faithfully according to God's commandments.
  • Support the mission of the church.
  • Prepare daily for Christ's Second Coming.

Reflection Questions

1. What does it mean to be part of God's remnant people?

2. How can I participate in God's end-time mission?

3. Am I helping others prepare for Christ's return?
