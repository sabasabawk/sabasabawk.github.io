💰 Lesson 9: Financial Stewardship in the Home

Key Text

"Moreover it is required in stewards, that a man be found faithful."

1 Corinthians 4:2

Lesson

Money is a gift from God, and every family is called to be a faithful steward of the resources they receive.

Good financial stewardship means using money wisely, avoiding debt where possible, and prioritizing needs over wants.

A Christian family should plan their spending, save regularly, and give generously to God’s work and those in need.

When families manage money according to biblical principles, they experience peace, stability, and fewer conflicts.

The Seventh-day Adventist teaching emphasizes honesty, simplicity, and faithfulness in financial matters as part of Christian living.

Principles of Financial Stewardship

  • Recognize God as the owner of all things.
  • Create a simple family budget.
  • Avoid unnecessary debt.
  • Save for future needs.
  • Give tithes and offerings faithfully.
  • Practice honesty in all financial dealings.
