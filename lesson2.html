Lesson 2: Prayer

Main Scripture

Jeremiah 33:3 — “Call unto me, and I will answer thee.”

Introduction

Prayer is one of the most important parts of the Christian life. Through prayer we communicate with God, receive strength, guidance, peace, and spiritual growth.

What Is Prayer?

Prayer is speaking with God sincerely from the heart through faith in Jesus Christ.

Why Prayer Is Important

  • Prayer brings us closer to God
  • Prayer strengthens our faith
  • Prayer gives peace during difficult times
  • Prayer helps believers overcome temptation

Jesus Taught About Prayer

Jesus taught His disciples to pray faithfully and sincerely.

Matthew 6:6 — “When you pray, go into your room, close the door and pray to your Father.”

Types of Prayer

  • Thanksgiving
  • Worship
  • Repentance
  • Intercession
  • Prayer for needs

How To Pray Effectively

  • Pray with faith
  • Pray sincerely
  • Pray consistently
  • Pray according to God's will

Conclusion

A strong Christian life cannot exist without prayer. Prayer opens the door for God's power to work in our lives.
