🌍 Lesson 9: Stewardship and Mission

Key Text

"Go ye therefore, and teach all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost."

Matthew 28:19

Introduction

God has entrusted every believer with a mission. Christian stewardship is not limited to money, time, or talents; it also includes participating in God's work of saving souls.

Every Christian has a role in sharing the gospel and helping others prepare for the soon return of Jesus Christ.

The Great Commission

Before ascending to heaven, Jesus gave His followers the Great Commission. He instructed them to take the gospel to the entire world.

This mission continues today. Every believer is called to be a witness for Christ through words, actions, and personal example.

Using Our Gifts for Mission

God provides various resources that can be used for mission:

  • Time for witnessing and service.
  • Talents for ministry.
  • Money to support evangelism.
  • Knowledge to teach others.
  • Influence to lead people to Christ.

Faithful stewards dedicate their resources to advancing God's kingdom.

Mission Begins at Home

Mission work often starts with family members, friends, neighbors, and coworkers. A kind word, a Bible study, a prayer, or an act of service can open hearts to the gospel.

Every Christian can be a missionary wherever God has placed them.

The Joy of Soul Winning

There is great joy in helping others know Jesus. Heaven rejoices when people accept Christ and choose to follow Him.

Faithful stewards seek opportunities to share God's love and truth with the world.

Memory Verse

"But ye shall receive power, after that the Holy Ghost is come upon you: and ye shall be witnesses unto me."

Acts 1:8
