Fundamental Belief #21

Stewardship

Key Bible Texts

Genesis 1:26-28

Malachi 3:8-10

Matthew 25:14-30

1 Corinthians 4:2

Colossians 3:23-24

What We Believe

We are God's stewards, entrusted by Him with time, opportunities, abilities, possessions, and the blessings of the earth and its resources. We are responsible to Him for their proper use.

We acknowledge God's ownership by faithful service to Him and our fellow human beings, and by returning tithes and giving offerings for the proclamation of His Gospel and the support and growth of His church.

Stewardship is a privilege given to us by God for nurture in love and the victory over selfishness and covetousness. Faithful stewards rejoice in the blessings that result to others because of their faithfulness.

Areas of Christian Stewardship

  • Time: Use time wisely for God and service.
  • Talents: Develop and use gifts for God's glory.
  • Health: Care for the body as God's temple.
  • Possessions: Manage resources faithfully.
  • Finances: Return faithful tithe and offerings.
  • Environment: Care for God's creation responsibly.

Why It Matters

Stewardship recognizes that everything belongs to God. It teaches gratitude, faithfulness, generosity, and dependence upon Him. Faithful stewardship helps advance God's mission and blesses others.

Practical Application

  • Return a faithful tithe.
  • Support God's work through offerings.
  • Use your talents in ministry.
  • Take care of your health.
  • Manage money wisely.
  • Use time purposefully.
  • Help those in need.
  • Protect God's creation.

Reflection Questions

1. Am I managing God's blessings faithfully?

2. How can I better use my talents for God?

3. What changes can I make to become a more faithful steward?
