⛪ Lesson 7: Leading in the Church

Key Text

"Feed the flock of God which is among you, taking the oversight thereof, not by constraint, but willingly... neither as being lords over God's heritage, but being ensamples to the flock."

1 Peter 5:2–3

Introduction

Leadership in the church is a sacred responsibility. Every church leader is called to serve Christ by caring for His people with love, humility, and faithfulness. Church leadership is not about power but about serving God's family.

1. Jesus Is the Head of the Church

Every church leader serves under the authority of Jesus Christ. Our purpose is to honor Him in every decision and every ministry.

2. Lead by Example

Members watch the lives of their leaders. A church leader should demonstrate honesty, kindness, humility, faithfulness, and love in both public and private life.

3. Serve Willingly

Church work should never be done for praise, position, or personal gain. Christian leaders serve joyfully because they love God and His people.

4. Promote Unity

Leaders should encourage peace, cooperation, forgiveness, and unity among church members. Unity strengthens the church and glorifies God.

5. Depend on the Holy Spirit

No leader can successfully serve without God's guidance. Prayer, Bible study, and dependence upon the Holy Spirit are essential for effective ministry.

Lesson Summary

  • Jesus Christ is the Head of the Church.
  • Church leaders are servants, not rulers.
  • Leaders must be examples to others.
  • Unity is one of the leader's responsibilities.
  • The Holy Spirit empowers Christian leadership.
