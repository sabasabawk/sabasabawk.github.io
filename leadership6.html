🏡 Lesson 6: Leading Your Family

Key Text

"But as for me and my house, we will serve the Lord."

Joshua 24:15

Introduction

The first place where leadership is practiced is not the church or the workplace—it is the home. God calls every Christian to be an example of faith, love, and integrity within the family.

1. Leadership Begins at Home

A person who faithfully leads their family develops qualities that prepare them to serve others. The home is God's training ground for leadership.

2. Lead by Example

Family members learn more from what they see than from what they hear. A godly leader demonstrates honesty, kindness, forgiveness, patience, and faith in daily life.

3. Build a Christ-Centered Home

Make prayer, Bible study, and worship regular parts of family life. Encourage every family member to grow in their relationship with Jesus.

4. Love and Respect One Another

Christian leadership is expressed through love, respect, encouragement, and selfless service. Strong families are built on mutual care and biblical values.

5. Leave a Godly Legacy

The greatest inheritance we can leave our children is a life of faithfulness to God. A godly example influences future generations.

Lesson Summary

  • Leadership begins in the home.
  • Godly leaders lead by example.
  • Prayer and Bible study strengthen families.
  • Love and respect build strong homes.
  • A faithful life leaves a lasting legacy.
