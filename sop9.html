🌿 Lesson 9: Health Reform and the Spirit of Prophecy

Key Text

"Beloved, I wish above all things that thou mayest prosper and be in health, even as thy soul prospereth."

3 John 1:2

Lesson Study

One of the most influential areas of Ellen G. White's ministry was health reform. Through visions and inspired counsel, she encouraged God's people to care for their bodies as temples of the Holy Spirit.

In 1863, Ellen White received an important vision on health. She emphasized the connection between physical health, mental well-being, and spiritual growth.

Her writings encouraged proper nutrition, exercise, fresh air, pure water, sunlight, rest, temperance, and trust in God. These principles later became known as part of the Adventist health message.

The Spirit of Prophecy teaches that good health enables people to serve God more effectively and enjoy a better quality of life.

Today, Adventist hospitals, clinics, and health ministries around the world continue to apply these principles, helping millions of people live healthier lives.

Principles of Health Reform

  • Eat wholesome and nutritious foods.
  • Exercise regularly.
  • Drink pure water.
  • Get adequate rest.
  • Enjoy fresh air and sunlight.
  • Avoid harmful substances.
  • Trust in God's healing power.
