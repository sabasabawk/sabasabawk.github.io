Fundamental Belief #5

The Holy Spirit

Key Bible Texts

John 14:16-17

John 16:13

Acts 1:8

Ephesians 4:30

What We Believe

God the eternal Spirit was active with the Father and the Son in Creation, the incarnation, and redemption. He inspired the writers of Scripture and filled Christ's life with power. He draws people to God and convicts them of sin, righteousness, and judgment.

The Holy Spirit renews and transforms believers into the image of Christ. He dwells within God's people, guides them into truth, empowers them for service, and gives spiritual gifts for the building up of the church.

Through the Holy Spirit, Christians experience God's presence, receive strength for daily living, and grow in faith and obedience.

Why It Matters

Without the Holy Spirit, we cannot fully understand God's Word or experience spiritual growth. The Spirit helps us overcome temptation, develop Christian character, and live a life that honors God.

Practical Application

  • Pray daily for the guidance of the Holy Spirit.
  • Study the Bible with an open and teachable heart.
  • Listen to God's conviction and obey His leading.
  • Use your spiritual gifts to serve others.
  • Allow the Spirit to produce Christlike character in your life.

Reflection Questions

1. How has the Holy Spirit guided me recently?

2. Am I listening when God speaks through His Spirit?

3. Which spiritual gifts can I use to bless others?
