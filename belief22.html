Fundamental Belief #22

Christian Behavior

Key Bible Texts

Romans 12:1-2

1 Corinthians 10:31

Philippians 4:8

1 Peter 1:15-16

Galatians 5:22-23

What We Believe

We are called to be a godly people who think, feel, and act in harmony with the principles of heaven. Through the Holy Spirit, Christ works within us to reproduce His character in our lives.

Christian behavior affects every area of life including our thoughts, speech, actions, relationships, recreation, entertainment, dress, health, and service to others. Followers of Christ seek to honor God in all they do.

Because our bodies are temples of the Holy Spirit, we care for our physical, mental, and spiritual well-being. We avoid practices and habits that harm us and seek to live lives that glorify God.

Characteristics of Christian Behavior

  • Love for God and others.
  • Honesty and integrity.
  • Purity in thought and action.
  • Kindness and compassion.
  • Humility and self-control.
  • Faithfulness and obedience.
  • Respect for others.
  • A commitment to holy living.

Why It Matters

Christian behavior is a witness to the world. As believers reflect the character of Jesus, others are drawn to Him. A Christ-centered life brings joy, peace, and spiritual growth.

Practical Application

  • Choose entertainment that honors God.
  • Practice healthy living.
  • Speak words that encourage others.
  • Dress modestly and respectfully.
  • Serve others with love.
  • Spend time daily in prayer and Bible study.
  • Allow the Holy Spirit to guide decisions.

Reflection Questions

1. Does my lifestyle reflect the character of Christ?

2. What habits should I strengthen or change?

3. How can I be a better witness for Jesus?
