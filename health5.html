🏃 Lesson 5: Exercise for Health

Key Text

"For bodily exercise profiteth little: but godliness is profitable unto all things..."

1 Timothy 4:8

Lesson

Exercise is one of God's natural remedies for maintaining health. Regular physical activity strengthens the heart, muscles, lungs, and bones while improving overall well-being.

Exercise helps reduce stress, improves sleep, boosts energy, and supports mental health. Walking, running, cycling, swimming, gardening, and other physical activities are excellent forms of exercise.

The Adventist Health Message encourages regular exercise as part of a balanced lifestyle. Physical activity helps us remain strong and fit for service to God and others.

While physical exercise is important, spiritual growth is even more important. Christians should seek both physical health and spiritual health.

Benefits of Exercise

  • Strengthens the heart and lungs.
  • Improves circulation.
  • Increases energy levels.
  • Helps maintain healthy weight.
  • Reduces stress and anxiety.
  • Improves sleep quality.

Memory Verse

"I can do all things through Christ which strengtheneth me."

Philippians 4:13
