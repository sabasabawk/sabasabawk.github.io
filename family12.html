🌍 Lesson 12: The Family as a Mission Center

Key Text

"Go ye therefore, and teach all nations... teaching them to observe all things whatsoever I have commanded you..."

Matthew 28:19-20

Lesson

God designed the family not only to be a place of love and growth, but also a center of mission. Every Christian home should shine as a light to the world.

A mission-centered family shares the love of Jesus through kindness, service, hospitality, and witnessing to neighbors and friends.

Parents should train children to become active participants in God’s mission by teaching them to pray for others, help the needy, and share their faith.

When Christ is the center of the home, the family naturally becomes a powerful witness of His transforming grace.

The Seventh-day Adventist Church encourages every home to become a small mission field preparing souls for the soon return of Jesus Christ.

Principles of a Mission Family

  • Make Christ the center of the home.
  • Pray for others daily.
  • Share God’s love through actions.
  • Practice hospitality and kindness.
  • Teach children to witness.
  • Support church mission work.

Memory Verse

"Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works, and glorify your Father which is in heaven."

Matthew 5:16
