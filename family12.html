👑 Lesson 12: Preparing Families for Christ's Return

Key Text

"And what I say unto you I say unto all, Watch."

Mark 13:37

Lesson

The greatest hope of every Christian family is the Second Coming of Jesus Christ. The Bible promises that Jesus will return to take His faithful people home.

Preparing for Christ's return begins in the home. Families should pray together, study God's Word, worship faithfully, and encourage one another in the Christian walk.

Parents should teach their children to love Jesus and trust His promises. Family members should forgive one another, serve others, and remain faithful to God's commandments.

The Seventh-day Adventist Church emphasizes the blessed hope of Christ's soon return. Christian homes should be places where this hope is taught, lived, and shared.

A family that follows Christ together will be ready to welcome Him when He comes in glory.

How Families Can Prepare

  • Maintain daily family worship.
  • Study the Bible regularly.
  • Pray together.
  • Attend church faithfully.
  • Share the gospel with others.
  • Live according to God's commandments.
  • Keep the hope of Christ's return alive.

Memory Verse

John 14:1-3
