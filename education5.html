🏡 Lesson 5: The Home as the First School

Key Text

"Train up a child in the way he should go: and when he is old, he will not depart from it."

Proverbs 22:6

Introduction

Long before children enter a classroom, they begin learning at home. Parents are the first teachers appointed by God. The values, habits, faith, and attitudes learned in the home often shape a person's entire life. Seventh-day Adventist education recognizes the home as the foundation of all true education.

1. Parents Are the First Teachers

God has given parents the responsibility of teaching their children to love Him, obey His commandments, and live according to biblical principles.

2. Teaching by Example

Children learn more from what they see than from what they hear. Parents should demonstrate honesty, kindness, prayer, respect, and faith in daily life.

3. Worship in the Home

Family worship strengthens faith. Reading the Bible, praying together, and singing hymns help children build a personal relationship with God.

4. Teaching Responsibility

Children should learn responsibility through household duties, respect for others, careful use of resources, and faithful service.

5. Cooperation Between Home and School

Parents and Christian teachers should work together to help children grow spiritually, mentally, physically, and socially.

Lesson Summary

  • The home is the first school.
  • Parents are God's first teachers.
  • Children learn through example.
  • Family worship builds faith.
  • Home and school should work together.
