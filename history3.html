SDA History Lesson 3

The Great Disappointment of 1844

What Was the Great Disappointment?

The Great Disappointment occurred on October 22, 1844, when thousands of Millerites expected Jesus Christ to return to earth. They had sincerely studied Bible prophecy and believed the 2300-day prophecy of Daniel 8:14 would end on that date.

When Jesus did not appear, many believers experienced deep sorrow, confusion, and disappointment.

Why Were They Disappointed?

The Millerites correctly understood the prophetic time period but misunderstood the event that would occur at its conclusion.

They believed the cleansing of the sanctuary referred to the purification of the earth by fire at Christ's Second Coming. Later Bible study revealed that the prophecy pointed instead to Christ's ministry in the heavenly sanctuary.

Faith After Disappointment

Many people abandoned the Advent Movement after October 22, 1844. However, a small group continued studying the Scriptures and seeking God's guidance.

These believers were determined to discover where their understanding had been mistaken while holding firmly to the truth of Bible prophecy.

Hiram Edson's Experience

Shortly after the disappointment, Hiram Edson experienced a deeper understanding of Christ's work in the heavenly sanctuary.

He and other believers concluded that Jesus had entered a new phase of His heavenly ministry rather than returning to earth in 1844.

This discovery became one of the foundations of Seventh-day Adventist doctrine.

Lessons Learned

  • God's Word can be trusted.
  • Sincere believers can misunderstand prophecy.
  • Disappointment can lead to deeper truth.
  • Faith should rest on Scripture, not feelings.
  • God continued leading His people after 1844.

Importance to SDA History

The Great Disappointment was a turning point that led believers to a clearer understanding of Christ's ministry in the heavenly sanctuary, the investigative judgment, and other important biblical teachings.

From this small group eventually emerged the Seventh-day Adventist Church.
