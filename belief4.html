Fundamental Belief #4

The Son

Key Bible Texts

John 1:1-3

John 3:16

Colossians 1:15-17

Hebrews 1:1-3

What We Believe

God the eternal Son became incarnate in Jesus Christ. Through Him all things were created, the character of God was revealed, the salvation of humanity was accomplished, and the world is judged.

Forever truly God, He became also truly human. He was conceived by the Holy Spirit and born of the virgin Mary. He lived and experienced temptation as a human being, yet perfectly exemplified the righteousness and love of God.

By His death on the cross, Jesus provided the atoning sacrifice for sin. Through His resurrection, He triumphed over evil and death, offering eternal life to all who believe in Him.

Why It Matters

Jesus Christ is the center of the Christian faith. Through Him we receive forgiveness, salvation, hope, and the promise of eternal life. His example teaches us how to live in obedience, love, and service to God.

Practical Application

  • Accept Jesus Christ as Lord and Savior.
  • Study His life and teachings in the Gospels.
  • Follow His example of love and service.
  • Trust in His sacrifice for forgiveness of sins.
  • Share the good news of salvation with others.

Reflection Questions

1. What does Jesus' sacrifice mean to me personally?

2. How can I follow Christ more closely each day?

3. In what ways can I reflect His character to others?
