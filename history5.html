SDA History Lesson 5

Ellen G. White and the Prophetic Gift

Who Was Ellen G. White?

Ellen Gould White was one of the founders of the Seventh-day Adventist Church. She was born on November 26, 1827, in Gorham, Maine, USA.

From her youth, she was active in the Advent Movement and became one of its most influential leaders.

The First Vision

In December 1844, shortly after the Great Disappointment, Ellen White received her first vision from God.

The vision encouraged disappointed Advent believers and assured them that God was still leading His people.

Throughout her life she received many visions and messages that strengthened the Advent movement.

The Gift of Prophecy

Seventh-day Adventists believe that Ellen White exercised the biblical gift of prophecy described in Scripture.

Her writings consistently pointed people to Jesus Christ, the Bible, prayer, evangelism, healthful living, and Christian growth.

She never claimed that her writings replaced the Bible. Instead, she taught that the Bible is the supreme authority for faith and practice.

Her Major Contributions

  • Encouraged worldwide evangelism.
  • Supported Christian education.
  • Promoted health and medical ministry.
  • Strengthened biblical understanding.
  • Helped organize the growing church.
  • Encouraged missionary work around the world.

Important Books

  • Steps to Christ
  • The Desire of Ages
  • The Great Controversy
  • Patriarchs and Prophets
  • Prophets and Kings
  • Acts of the Apostles
  • Education

Legacy

Ellen White became one of the most translated female authors in history. Her books continue to inspire millions of people around the world.

She died on July 16, 1915, but her influence continues through her writings and the worldwide ministry of the Seventh-day Adventist Church.

Importance to SDA History

Ellen White helped guide the developing Adventist Church during its early years. Her ministry encouraged faithfulness to Scripture, commitment to mission, and preparation for the Second Coming of Christ.
