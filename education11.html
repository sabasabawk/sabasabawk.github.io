🌟 Lesson 11: Preparing for Service in This World and the Next

Key Text

"Even as the Son of man came not to be ministered unto, but to minister, and to give his life a ransom for many."

Matthew 20:28

Introduction

The goal of Seventh-day Adventist education is not simply to prepare students for employment, but to prepare them for a life of joyful service. Jesus lived a life of service, and His followers are called to use their knowledge, talents, and abilities to bless others while preparing for His soon return.

1. Jesus Is Our Example of Service

Christ used His life to teach, heal, encourage, and save others. Every Christian should seek opportunities to serve with love and humility.

2. Every Talent Is a Gift from God

God gives different abilities to every person. Whether teaching, farming, medicine, business, engineering, preaching, or any other profession, our gifts should be used for God's glory.

3. Service Is Part of Christian Education

Students should learn to help their families, churches, schools, and communities. Service develops compassion, responsibility, and leadership.

4. Witness Through Daily Life

Our actions often speak louder than our words. Honest work, kindness, respect, and integrity allow others to see Christ in us.

5. Preparing for Eternity

Every lesson we learn should help prepare us for Christ's second coming. True education points beyond this world to God's everlasting kingdom.

Lesson Summary

  • Jesus is the perfect example of service.
  • Every talent should be used for God's glory.
  • Christian education prepares people to serve others.
  • Our daily lives should witness for Christ.
  • The final goal is preparation for eternal life.
