SDA History Lesson 8

Medical Missionary Work

Introduction

One of the unique contributions of the Seventh-day Adventist Church is its health and medical ministry. Early Adventists believed that caring for the body is an important part of Christian living and service to God.

The Adventist health message emphasizes physical, mental, social, and spiritual well-being.

The Health Vision of 1863

In 1863, shortly after the organization of the church, Ellen G. White received important counsel regarding healthful living.

This instruction encouraged believers to avoid harmful habits and adopt health practices that promote longer and healthier lives.

Principles of the Adventist Health Message

  • Trust in God.
  • Regular exercise.
  • Balanced nutrition.
  • Fresh air and sunlight.
  • Adequate rest.
  • Clean water.
  • Temperance and self-control.

These principles are often remembered through the acronym NEWSTART: Nutrition, Exercise, Water, Sunlight, Temperance, Air, Rest, and Trust in God.

Battle Creek Sanitarium

One of the most famous Adventist medical institutions was the Battle Creek Sanitarium in Michigan, USA.

It became a leading center for health education, lifestyle medicine, and patient care.

John Harvey Kellogg

John Harvey Kellogg was a physician who served at Battle Creek Sanitarium. He helped promote health education and preventive medicine.

His work contributed to the growth of Adventist medical ministry, although some of his later teachings differed from official church beliefs.

Worldwide Medical Ministry

As the church expanded, hospitals, clinics, dispensaries, and health centers were established around the world.

  • Hospitals
  • Clinics
  • Medical schools
  • Health education programs
  • Community health outreach
  • Disaster and relief services

Importance to SDA History

Medical missionary work opened doors for evangelism and demonstrated Christ's method of ministry by meeting people's physical and spiritual needs.

Today the Adventist Church operates one of the largest Protestant healthcare systems in the world.
