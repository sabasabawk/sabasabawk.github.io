Lesson 3: Faith

Main Scripture

Hebrews 11:1 — “Now faith is the substance of things hoped for, the evidence of things not seen.”

Introduction

Faith is the foundation of the Christian life. Through faith believers trust God and walk according to His Word.

What Is Faith?

Faith is believing and trusting God even when we cannot physically see the answer.

Why Faith Matters

Without faith it is impossible to please God.

Hebrews 11:6 — “But without faith it is impossible to please Him.”

Examples of Faith in the Bible

  • Abraham trusted God
  • Noah built the ark by faith
  • David defeated Goliath through faith
  • The woman with the issue of blood believed Jesus could heal her

How To Grow Your Faith

  • Read the Word of God
  • Pray daily
  • Obey God
  • Stay connected to believers

Romans 10:17 — “Faith comes by hearing, and hearing by the word of God.”

Conclusion

Faith gives believers victory over fear, doubt, and challenges. Through faith we walk with God confidently.
