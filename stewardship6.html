💪 Lesson 6: Stewardship of the Body

Key Text

"What? know ye not that your body is the temple of the Holy Ghost which is in you, which ye have of God, and ye are not your own?"

1 Corinthians 6:19

Introduction

God has entrusted each of us with a body. Just as we are called to manage our money, time, and talents faithfully, we are also called to care for our physical health.

The Bible teaches that our bodies are temples of the Holy Spirit. Therefore, we should honor God by maintaining good health and avoiding practices that harm the body.

The Body as God's Temple

Because God created us and redeemed us through Christ, our bodies belong to Him. We are stewards of our health and should seek to preserve our physical, mental, and spiritual well-being.

Good health enables us to serve God more effectively and enjoy a better quality of life.

Biblical Principles of Health

  • Eat wholesome and nutritious foods.
  • Drink plenty of clean water.
  • Exercise regularly.
  • Get sufficient rest and sleep.
  • Maintain personal cleanliness.
  • Trust in God through prayer.
  • Avoid harmful substances.

The Adventist Health Message

Seventh-day Adventists believe that health is an important part of Christian living. Through healthy habits, believers can better reflect God's character and serve humanity.

The Adventist health message emphasizes balanced living, temperance, proper nutrition, exercise, fresh air, sunlight, water, rest, and trust in God.

Glorifying God Through Health

Healthy living is not merely about living longer; it is about honoring God. Our choices should demonstrate respect for the gift of life and the body God has entrusted to us.

Memory Verse

"Whether therefore ye eat, or drink, or whatsoever ye do, do all to the glory of God."

1 Corinthians 10:31
