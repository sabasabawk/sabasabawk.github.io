😴 Lesson 6: Rest and Sleep

Key Text

"It is vain for you to rise up early, to sit up late... for so he giveth his beloved sleep."

Psalm 127:2

Lesson

God designed our bodies to need regular rest and sleep. Sleep allows the body and mind to recover from daily activities and prepare for a new day.

Lack of sleep can lead to fatigue, poor concentration, weakened immunity, stress, and health problems. Most adults need about 7–9 hours of sleep each night.

The Sabbath is also God's special gift of rest. By setting aside the seventh day for worship and fellowship with Him, we receive physical, mental, and spiritual renewal.

Good sleep habits include going to bed at a regular time, avoiding unnecessary late-night activities, and trusting God with the concerns of life.

Benefits of Rest and Sleep

  • Restores energy.
  • Improves memory and concentration.
  • Strengthens the immune system.
  • Supports emotional well-being.
  • Promotes overall health.

Memory Verse

Matthew 11:28
