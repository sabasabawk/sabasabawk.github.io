👑 Lesson 1: What Is Biblical Leadership?

Key Text

"But he that is greatest among you shall be your servant."

Matthew 23:11

Introduction

Many people think leadership is about power, position, or authority. The Bible teaches something different. A true leader serves others, honors God, and seeks the good of those under his or her care.

1. Leadership Is a Calling

God calls men and women to lead in their homes, churches, schools, workplaces, and communities. Biblical leadership begins with a willingness to obey God.

2. Jesus Is Our Perfect Example

Jesus led through humility, love, compassion, and sacrifice. Although He was Lord of all, He served others faithfully.

3. Leaders Influence Others

Every Christian influences someone. Good leaders encourage others to follow Christ through their words, actions, and example.

4. Character Matters

A godly leader is honest, trustworthy, humble, prayerful, and faithful.

Lesson Summary

  • Leadership begins with God.
  • Jesus is the perfect Leader.
  • Leadership means serving others.
  • Godly character is more important than position.
