Fundamental Belief #10

The Experience of Salvation

Key Bible Texts

Ephesians 2:8-9

2 Corinthians 5:17

John 3:16

Romans 6:23

1 John 1:9

What We Believe

In infinite love and mercy, God made Christ, who knew no sin, to be sin for us, so that in Him we might be made righteous before God. Through the Holy Spirit we sense our need, acknowledge our sinfulness, repent of our transgressions, and exercise faith in Jesus Christ as Lord and Saviour.

This saving faith comes through the divine power of the Word and is the gift of God's grace. Through Christ we are justified, adopted as God's sons and daughters, and delivered from the lordship of sin.

Through the Spirit we are born again and sanctified. The Spirit renews our minds, writes God's law of love in our hearts, and empowers us to live a holy life.

Why It Matters

Salvation is the heart of the Gospel. It gives forgiveness for past sins, power for present living, and hope for eternal life. Through Jesus Christ we become new creations and enter into a personal relationship with God.

Practical Application

  • Accept Jesus Christ as your personal Savior.
  • Confess your sins and seek God's forgiveness.
  • Study God's Word daily.
  • Allow the Holy Spirit to transform your character.
  • Live each day by faith in Christ.

Reflection Questions

1. Have I fully accepted God's gift of salvation?

2. What changes has Christ made in my life?

3. How can I grow closer to Jesus every day?
