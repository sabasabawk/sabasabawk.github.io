💪 Lesson 9: Health, Work, and Practical Education

Key Text

"Whatsoever ye do, do it heartily, as to the Lord, and not unto men."

Colossians 3:23

Introduction

Seventh-day Adventist education teaches that true education includes more than classroom learning. God desires His children to care for their health, develop practical skills, and value honest work. Physical labor, healthy living, and useful service are all important parts of Christian education.

1. Our Bodies Are God's Temple

The Bible teaches that our bodies belong to God. Good health enables us to think clearly, serve faithfully, and honor our Creator.

"Know ye not that your body is the temple of the Holy Ghost?"

1 Corinthians 6:19–20

2. The Dignity of Work

Work was given before sin entered the world. Honest labor develops discipline, responsibility, and respect for others. Every useful occupation can glorify God.

3. Practical Skills Are Important

Students should learn useful life skills such as gardening, craftsmanship, stewardship, communication, and service. Practical education prepares people for everyday life.

4. Balance Mental and Physical Activity

Healthy minds and healthy bodies work together. Regular exercise, proper rest, nutritious food, and productive work contribute to successful learning.

5. Serve Others Through Your Talents

God gives every person abilities to bless others. Education should prepare students to use their knowledge and skills in service to their families, communities, church, and the world.

Lesson Summary

  • Our bodies are God's temple.
  • Honest work honors God.
  • Practical skills are valuable.
  • Health supports effective learning.
  • Education prepares us for useful service.
