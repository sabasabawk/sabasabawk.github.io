Fundamental Belief #27

The Millennium and the End of Sin

Key Bible Texts

Revelation 20:1-10

1 Corinthians 6:2-3

Jeremiah 4:23-26

Malachi 4:1

Revelation 21:1-4

What We Believe

The millennium is the thousand-year reign of Christ with His saints in heaven between the first and second resurrections.

During this time, the righteous will participate in reviewing God's judgments, while Satan is confined to the desolated earth with no one to tempt. The earth remains empty and without human inhabitants.

At the close of the thousand years, Christ, the redeemed, and the Holy City descend from heaven to earth. The wicked dead are then resurrected and join Satan in a final rebellion against God.

Fire comes down from God and completely destroys Satan, his angels, and all evil. Sin and sinners are forever removed from the universe. God's justice and love are fully revealed.

Events of the Millennium

  1. Jesus returns and takes the righteous to heaven.
  2. The wicked living are destroyed.
  3. Satan remains on the desolate earth for 1,000 years.
  4. The saints participate in the judgment.
  5. The Holy City descends from heaven.
  6. The wicked are resurrected.
  7. Satan leads a final rebellion.
  8. Fire destroys sin, Satan, and all evil forever.

Why It Matters

The millennium demonstrates God's fairness and justice. It gives the redeemed an opportunity to understand God's judgments and confirms that His ways are true and righteous.

Practical Application

  • Trust God's justice and wisdom.
  • Remain faithful to Christ.
  • Share the Gospel while there is opportunity.
  • Look forward to a universe free from sin.
  • Live with hope in God's final victory.

Reflection Questions

1. What does the millennium teach about God's justice?

2. How does God's final victory over sin encourage me?

3. Am I preparing for eternity with Christ?
