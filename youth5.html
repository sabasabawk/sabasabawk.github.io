👥 Youth Lesson 5: Choosing Good Friends

Key Text

"He that walketh with wise men shall be wise: but a companion of fools shall be destroyed."

Proverbs 13:20

Introduction

Friendships are one of the greatest influences in a young person's life. The people we spend time with can shape our attitudes, choices, habits, and even our relationship with God.

God wants us to choose friends who encourage us to do what is right and help us grow spiritually.

The Power of Friendship

Friends influence the way we think, speak, and act. Good friends inspire us to become better people, while bad influences can lead us away from God's plan.

This is why the Bible emphasizes the importance of wise friendships.

Qualities of Good Friends

  • They respect God and His Word.
  • They are honest and trustworthy.
  • They encourage good decisions.
  • They show kindness and respect.
  • They support you during difficult times.

Warning Signs of Bad Influences

  • Encouraging disobedience.
  • Promoting harmful habits.
  • Mocking spiritual values.
  • Creating unnecessary conflict.
  • Pressuring others to do wrong.

Jesus and Friendship

Jesus chose friends who were willing to learn from Him and follow His teachings. He loved people deeply while still standing firmly for truth.

As Christians, we should be friendly to everyone while choosing our closest friends carefully.

Being a Good Friend

Not only should we seek good friends, but we should also strive to be good friends ourselves.

  • Be honest.
  • Be loyal.
  • Be supportive.
  • Show forgiveness.
  • Lead others toward Christ.

Friendships and Spiritual Growth

Strong Christian friendships help us remain faithful during challenges. Friends can pray with us, encourage us, and help us stay focused on God's purpose.

Choose friendships that strengthen your faith rather than weaken it.
