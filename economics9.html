💰 Lesson 9: Honesty and Business Ethics

Key Text

"A false balance is abomination to the Lord: but a just weight is his delight."

Proverbs 11:1

Introduction

God expects Christians to be honest in every financial activity. Whether buying, selling, managing a business, or working for someone else, integrity should always guide our decisions.

What Is Business Ethics?

Business ethics are moral principles that guide how we earn, spend, and manage money. Biblical business ethics are based on truthfulness, fairness, respect, and love for others.

God Hates Dishonesty

Throughout the Bible, God condemns cheating, stealing, corruption, bribery, false advertising, and dishonest measurements.

A Christian should never gain wealth through deception.

Characteristics of an Honest Business Person

  • Tells the truth.
  • Uses honest weights and measurements.
  • Keeps promises.
  • Pays workers fairly.
  • Charges fair prices.
  • Avoids corruption and bribery.
  • Treats customers with respect.

The Blessings of Integrity

People trust honest individuals. Integrity builds a good reputation, strengthens relationships, and brings God's blessing upon our work.

"Better is a little with righteousness than great revenues without right."

Proverbs 16:8

Jesus Our Example

Jesus always acted with honesty, compassion, and fairness. As His followers, we should reflect His character in every business transaction.

Application

Examine your daily life. Are you honest in school, at work, in business, and when handling money? Ask God to help you become a person of unquestionable integrity.
