💍 Marriage Lesson 9: Raising Children God's Way

Key Text

"Train up a child in the way he should go: and when he is old, he will not depart from it."

Proverbs 22:6

Introduction

Children are a precious gift from God. Parents have the sacred responsibility of guiding, teaching, protecting, and preparing their children to become faithful followers of Christ.

Raising children is one of the greatest ministries God gives to families.

Children Are a Blessing

The Bible describes children as a heritage from the Lord. They should be loved, valued, and cared for as gifts entrusted to parents by God.

Every child has unique talents and abilities that should be nurtured.

The Role of Parents

Parents are the first teachers their children will know. Children learn not only from what parents say but also from what they do.

  • Teach God's Word.
  • Set a good example.
  • Pray with and for your children.
  • Provide loving discipline.
  • Encourage honesty and responsibility.

Teaching Spiritual Values

Children should learn about God from an early age. Family worship, Bible study, prayer, and Christian service help build a strong spiritual foundation.

Parents should create a home where faith is practiced daily.

The Importance of Discipline

Biblical discipline is motivated by love, not anger. Discipline helps children learn self-control, respect, responsibility, and obedience.

Correction should always be fair, consistent, and aimed at helping the child grow.

Building Strong Relationships

Children thrive when they feel loved and accepted. Parents should spend quality time with their children and maintain open communication.

  • Listen carefully.
  • Show affection.
  • Encourage their efforts.
  • Celebrate achievements.
  • Provide guidance during challenges.

Preparing Children for Life

Parents should teach practical life skills, strong character, and dependence on God. The goal is to prepare children to become responsible adults who honor Christ.

A Christ-Centered Home

The most effective parenting takes place in a Christ-centered home. When children see their parents loving God and living by biblical principles, they are more likely to follow the same path.

Memory Verse
