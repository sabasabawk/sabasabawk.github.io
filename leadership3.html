👑 Lesson 3: Servant Leadership

Key Text

"Whosoever will be chief among you, let him be your servant."

Matthew 20:27

Introduction

The world often teaches that leadership means being in control and having authority over others. Jesus taught the opposite. The greatest leaders are those who willingly serve others with humility and love.

1. A Servant Leader Puts Others First

Servant leaders care about the needs of others. They seek to encourage, help, and build people up instead of seeking personal recognition.

2. Jesus Was the Perfect Servant Leader

Jesus demonstrated servant leadership by healing the sick, feeding the hungry, comforting the sorrowful, and washing His disciples' feet.

3. Humility Is Essential

Pride destroys leadership, but humility earns respect. Christian leaders recognize that every gift and opportunity comes from God.

4. Serve With Love

Leadership without love becomes harsh and selfish. God's leaders serve with kindness, patience, compassion, and forgiveness.

5. Leadership Is Ministry

Whether leading in the church, home, workplace, or community, Christian leadership is an opportunity to glorify God through faithful service.

Lesson Summary

  • True leaders serve others.
  • Jesus is our example of servant leadership.
  • Humility is a mark of great leadership.
  • Love should guide every leader.
