💰 Lesson 6: Debt and Borrowing

Key Text

"The rich ruleth over the poor, and the borrower is servant to the lender."

Proverbs 22:7

Introduction

The Bible does not completely forbid borrowing, but it warns that debt can become a burden. Christians are encouraged to live wisely, avoid unnecessary debt, and trust God to provide for their needs.

What Is Debt?

Debt is money or property borrowed from another person or institution with the promise to repay it later.

While some debt may be unavoidable, unnecessary borrowing can lead to financial stress and loss of freedom.

Biblical Warnings About Debt

  • Debt can make a person financially dependent.
  • Debt may create anxiety and family problems.
  • Failure to repay damages one's Christian witness.
  • Living beyond your means often leads to debt.

How to Avoid Unnecessary Debt

  • Prepare and follow a budget.
  • Save before making large purchases.
  • Avoid impulse buying.
  • Be content with what God has provided.
  • Pray before making financial decisions.

Repaying What You Owe

Christians should be known for honesty and integrity. If you borrow money, make every effort to repay it faithfully and on time.

Psalm 37:21 says, "The wicked borroweth, and payeth not again: but the righteous sheweth mercy, and giveth."

Trusting God

Instead of depending on debt, believers are encouraged to trust God, work diligently, manage resources wisely, and seek His guidance in every financial decision.

Application

Evaluate your financial situation. If you have debt, develop a plan to repay it responsibly. If you are debt-free, ask God to help you remain a faithful steward of His blessings.
