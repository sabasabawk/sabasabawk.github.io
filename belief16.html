Fundamental Belief #16

The Lord's Supper

Key Bible Texts

Luke 22:19-20

John 13:1-17

1 Corinthians 11:23-26

Matthew 26:26-29

What We Believe

The Lord's Supper is a participation in the emblems of the body and blood of Jesus Christ as an expression of faith in Him as our Lord and Savior. In this experience of communion, Christ is present through the Holy Spirit to meet and strengthen His people.

Preparation for the Lord's Supper includes self-examination, repentance, and confession. Christ instituted the service of foot washing to signify renewed cleansing, express willingness to serve one another in humility, and unite our hearts in love.

The bread and the fruit of the vine are symbols of Christ's body and blood. By partaking, believers remember His sacrifice, celebrate salvation, and proclaim His death until He comes again.

Why It Matters

The Lord's Supper reminds believers of Christ's sacrifice on the cross. It strengthens faith, promotes unity among believers, and renews our commitment to follow Jesus Christ faithfully.

Practical Application

  • Approach Communion with prayer and reverence.
  • Examine your relationship with God.
  • Forgive others and seek reconciliation.
  • Practice humility and Christian service.
  • Remember Christ's sacrifice with gratitude.

Reflection Questions

1. What does Christ's sacrifice mean to me?

2. Am I living in harmony with fellow believers?

3. How can I better serve others as Jesus did?
