👥 Youth Lesson 11: Leadership for Christ

Key Text

"And whosoever will be chief among you, let him be your servant."

Matthew 20:27

Introduction

Leadership is influence. Every young person influences someone through words, actions, and example. Christian leadership is different from worldly leadership because it is centered on serving others rather than seeking power or recognition.

God calls young people to become leaders who reflect the character of Jesus Christ.

Jesus: The Greatest Leader

Jesus is the perfect example of leadership. Although He was the Son of God, He humbled Himself and served others.

He taught that true greatness comes through service, sacrifice, and love.

Qualities of a Christian Leader

  • Faithfulness
  • Integrity
  • Humility
  • Wisdom
  • Compassion
  • Self-control
  • Courage
  • Dependence on God

Young Leaders in the Bible

God used many young leaders throughout Scripture:

  • Joseph led in Egypt.
  • David led Israel.
  • Daniel influenced Babylon.
  • Esther saved her people.
  • Timothy served the early church.

Their success came from trusting God rather than relying on themselves.

Leading by Example

People learn more from what leaders do than from what they say. A Christian leader should model honesty, kindness, faithfulness, and obedience to God.

Your example can inspire others to follow Christ.

Serving Others

Christian leadership is servant leadership. Instead of asking, "How can others serve me?" a Christian leader asks, "How can I serve others?"

Service is one of the greatest expressions of Christlike leadership.

Challenges of Leadership

  • Criticism
  • Responsibility
  • Difficult decisions
  • Temptation to pride
  • Discouragement

Through prayer and faith, God equips leaders to overcome these challenges.

Preparing for Leadership

  • Study God's Word.
  • Develop a prayer life.
  • Learn from godly mentors.
  • Practice serving others.
  • Remain teachable.

Memory Verse

"And whosoever will be chief among you, let him be your servant."

Matthew 20:27
