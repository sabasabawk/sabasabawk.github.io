🕊️ Lesson 10: Walking in God's Protection

Key Text

"He that dwelleth in the secret place of the most High shall abide under the shadow of the Almighty."

Psalm 91:1

Introduction

Throughout this course we have learned that God's people should avoid occult practices and place their trust entirely in Him.

The Christian life is not built on fear but on confidence in God's love, power, and protection.

God Is Our Refuge

Psalm 91 describes God as our refuge, fortress, and protector.

Those who trust in Him can live with peace even in difficult circumstances.

Protection Through Faith

God does not ask us to trust in charms, magical objects, lucky items, or superstitions.

He invites us to trust Him through faith and obedience.

Daily Spiritual Habits

  • Pray every day.
  • Study the Bible daily.
  • Attend church regularly.
  • Share your faith with others.
  • Trust God's promises.

The Power of God's Word

Jesus used Scripture to resist Satan during His temptation in the wilderness.

God's Word remains one of the strongest defenses against deception and temptation.

Living Without Fear

Christians do not need to live in fear of curses, witchcraft, evil spirits, or dark powers.

Jesus is greater than every power of darkness.

Looking Forward

Our ultimate hope is not merely protection in this life but eternal life through Jesus Christ.

One day Christ will return and completely destroy sin, evil, and suffering forever.

Course Summary

In this course you have learned:

  • What the Bible teaches about magic and witchcraft.
  • God's warnings against occult practices.
  • The stories of Simon the Sorcerer and the Witch of Endor.
  • The difference between God's power and magic.
  • Spiritual warfare and victory through Christ.
  • How to walk daily in God's protection.
