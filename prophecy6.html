📖 Lesson 6: Daniel's Vision of the Four Beasts

Key Text

"These great beasts, which are four, are four kings, which shall arise out of the earth."

Daniel 7:17

Introduction

In Daniel chapter 7, God gave Daniel a vision of four strange beasts rising from the sea.

These beasts represented kingdoms that would rule the world. This prophecy covers the same period as Daniel 2 but uses animals instead of metals.

The First Beast – A Lion

Daniel saw a lion with eagle's wings.

This beast represented Babylon, the same kingdom represented by the head of gold in Daniel 2.

The lion was a symbol of power and majesty.

The Second Beast – A Bear

The second beast was a bear raised up on one side with three ribs in its mouth.

This represented the Medo-Persian Empire which conquered Babylon.

The bear being raised on one side symbolized Persia becoming stronger than Media.

The Third Beast – A Leopard

Daniel then saw a leopard with four wings and four heads.

This represented Greece under Alexander the Great.

After Alexander's death, his empire was divided into four parts, symbolized by the four heads.

The Fourth Beast – Terrifying and Powerful

The fourth beast was unlike any other animal.

It had large iron teeth and was extremely powerful.

This beast represented Rome, the same empire symbolized by the legs of iron in Daniel 2.

The Ten Horns

Daniel saw ten horns on the fourth beast.

These represented the divisions of the Roman Empire after its fall.

The divided nations of Europe arose from these divisions.

The Little Horn

Among the ten horns, Daniel saw another small horn arise.

This little horn became very powerful and opposed God's truth.

Its full meaning will be studied in the next lesson.

The Heavenly Judgment

Daniel's vision shifted from earth to heaven.

He saw God's throne and the heavenly court.

The Ancient of Days sat in judgment while millions of angels stood before Him.

The Son of Man

Daniel saw Jesus, called the Son of Man, coming before the Ancient of Days.

Jesus received an everlasting kingdom that will never pass away.

Lessons for Today

  • God knows the future.
  • Prophecy confirms the reliability of the Bible.
  • Earthly kingdoms are temporary.
  • Christ's kingdom will last forever.

Application

World powers rise and fall, but God's kingdom remains eternal. Place your confidence in Jesus and His everlasting kingdom.
