Delete Your Faith Connect Account

Faith Connect respects your privacy. You can request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time.

How to request account deletion

  1. Open the Faith Connect app.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Select Delete Account if available, or contact us using the email below.
  4. Your request will be processed as soon as possible.

Data that will be deleted

  • Your account information
  • Your profile information
  • Your posts
  • Your comments
  • Your uploaded photos and videos
  • Your messages where applicable
