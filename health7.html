☀️ Lesson 7: Fresh Air and Sunshine

Key Text

"Truly the light is sweet, and a pleasant thing it is for the eyes to behold the sun."

Ecclesiastes 11:7

Lesson

God has provided fresh air and sunshine as important gifts for maintaining health. Clean air helps supply oxygen to every part of the body, while sunshine contributes to physical and emotional well-being.

Fresh air improves lung function, increases energy, and helps the body remove waste products efficiently. Spending time outdoors in clean environments can also reduce stress and improve mood.

Sunlight helps the body produce Vitamin D, which is important for strong bones, a healthy immune system, and overall wellness. Moderate exposure to sunshine is beneficial when practiced wisely.

The Adventist Health Message encourages spending time outdoors, enjoying God's creation, and appreciating the natural remedies He has provided.

Benefits of Fresh Air and Sunshine

  • Improves oxygen supply to the body.
  • Supports healthy lungs.
  • Boosts mood and emotional health.
  • Helps the body produce Vitamin D.
  • Encourages appreciation of God's creation.
