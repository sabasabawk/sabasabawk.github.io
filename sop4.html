📜 Lesson 4: The Authority of the Spirit of Prophecy

Key Text

"To the law and to the testimony: if they speak not according to this word, it is because there is no light in them."

Isaiah 8:20

Lesson Study

The Seventh-day Adventist Church teaches that the Bible is the supreme authority for faith and practice. All teachings, doctrines, and prophetic messages must be tested by Scripture.

The Spirit of Prophecy does not replace the Bible, nor does it add new doctrines. Instead, it helps believers understand and apply biblical truth in daily life.

Ellen G. White consistently pointed people to the Bible as God's inspired Word. She described her writings as a "lesser light" leading people to the "greater light," which is the Bible.

The authority of the Spirit of Prophecy comes from God, who gives spiritual gifts to His church. When the prophetic gift operates according to biblical tests, believers can benefit from its instruction, encouragement, and correction.

The writings of Ellen White provide practical guidance on Christian living, education, health, evangelism, stewardship, and preparation for Christ's return.

Important Principles

  • The Bible is the highest authority.
  • The Spirit of Prophecy supports Scripture.
  • True prophetic messages agree with God's Word.
  • The gift of prophecy strengthens the church.
  • Believers should study both the Bible and inspired counsel.
