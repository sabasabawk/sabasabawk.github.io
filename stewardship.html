💰 Christian Stewardship Course

Welcome

Christian stewardship is the joyful responsibility of managing God's gifts according to His will. Everything we have belongs to God, including our time, talents, money, health, relationships, and opportunities.

This 12-lesson course will help you understand biblical stewardship and prepare you to become a faithful steward for Christ.

📖 Key Verse: "The earth is the Lord's, and the fullness thereof." — Psalm 24:1
