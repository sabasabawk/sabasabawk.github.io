Fundamental Belief #19

The Law of God

Key Bible Texts

Exodus 20:1-17

Psalm 19:7-11

Matthew 5:17-18

Romans 7:12

James 2:10-12

What We Believe

The great principles of God's law are embodied in the Ten Commandments and exemplified in the life of Jesus Christ. They express God's love, will, and purpose concerning human conduct and relationships.

The law is holy, just, and good. It reveals God's character and serves as a standard of righteousness. Through the Holy Spirit, the law points out sin and helps people recognize their need of a Savior.

Salvation is by grace through faith and not by works, yet obedience to God's commandments is the fruit of a saving relationship with Jesus Christ. Believers keep God's law because they love Him and desire to follow His will.

The Ten Commandments

  1. No other gods before Me.
  2. No idols or images for worship.
  3. Do not misuse God's name.
  4. Remember the Sabbath day to keep it holy.
  5. Honor your father and mother.
  6. Do not murder.
  7. Do not commit adultery.
  8. Do not steal.
  9. Do not bear false witness.
  10. Do not covet.

Why It Matters

God's law provides guidance for a happy and meaningful life. It protects relationships, promotes justice, and helps believers live in harmony with God and others.

Practical Application

  • Study and understand the Ten Commandments.
  • Allow Christ to write His law in your heart.
  • Obey God out of love, not fear.
  • Reflect God's character in daily life.
  • Depend on God's grace for victory over sin.

Reflection Questions

1. What does God's law teach me about His character?

2. How does obedience demonstrate my love for God?

3. Which commandment speaks most strongly to my life today?
