📖 Lesson 12: The Woman, the Dragon, and the Remnant Church

Key Text

"And the dragon was wroth with the woman, and went to make war with the remnant of her seed."

Revelation 12:17

Introduction

Revelation 12 presents one of the most important prophetic chapters in the Bible.

It reveals the conflict between Christ and Satan and identifies God's faithful remnant people in the last days.

The Woman Clothed With the Sun

John saw a pure woman clothed with the sun, standing on the moon, and wearing a crown of twelve stars.

In Bible prophecy, a pure woman represents God's faithful church.

God has always had a people who remained loyal to Him.

The Male Child

The woman gave birth to a male child who would rule all nations.

This child represents Jesus Christ.

After His birth, Satan attempted to destroy Him, but Christ completed His mission and ascended to heaven.

The Great Red Dragon

The dragon represents Satan working through earthly powers.

Throughout history, Satan has opposed Christ and persecuted God's people.

The Woman in the Wilderness

The prophecy says the woman fled into the wilderness for 1260 prophetic days.

Using the prophetic day-year principle, this represents 1260 years.

During this period, God's faithful believers often worshiped in secret while facing persecution.

War in Heaven

John saw a great conflict in heaven.

Michael (Christ) and His angels fought against the dragon and his angels.

Satan was defeated and cast out.

The Remnant Church

At the end of the chapter, the dragon becomes angry with the remnant.

The remnant are God's faithful last-day people.

Revelation identifies them by two characteristics:

  • They keep the commandments of God.
  • They have the testimony of Jesus Christ.

The Testimony of Jesus

According to Revelation 19:10, the testimony of Jesus is the spirit of prophecy.

God promises special guidance for His people through His prophetic Word.

Lessons for Today

  • Satan opposes God's truth.
  • Jesus has already won the victory.
  • God always preserves a faithful people.
  • The remnant church is called to faithfulness and obedience.

Application

Choose to stand with Christ regardless of the pressures of the world. Remain faithful to God's commandments and trust in Jesus' victory.
