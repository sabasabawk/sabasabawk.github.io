Lesson 1: Salvation

Main Scripture

John 3:16

What Is Salvation?

Salvation is God's gift through Jesus Christ. Through salvation we receive forgiveness of sins and eternal life.

Why Do We Need Salvation?

All people have sinned and need God's mercy.

Romans 3:23 — “For all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God.”

How Do We Receive Salvation?

We receive salvation by believing in Jesus Christ and accepting Him as Lord and Savior.

Romans 10:9
