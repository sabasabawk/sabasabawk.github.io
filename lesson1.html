📜 History 1: The Protestant Reformation

Introduction

The Protestant Reformation was a major religious movement that began in Europe during the 16th century. It challenged certain teachings and practices of the medieval church and encouraged believers to return to the authority of the Bible.

Martin Luther

A German monk named Martin Luther became one of the leading figures of the Reformation. In 1517, he presented his concerns about church abuses, including the sale of indulgences. His actions sparked widespread religious discussion throughout Europe.

The Importance of the Bible

One of the key teachings of the Reformation was that the Bible should be the foundation of Christian faith and practice. Reformers encouraged people to study Scripture for themselves and trust God's Word.

Impact on Adventist History

The Protestant Reformation prepared the way for later movements that emphasized biblical truth, including the Advent Movement and the development of the Seventh-day Adventist Church.
