✝️ Lesson 8: Freedom Through Christ

Key Text

"If the Son therefore shall make you free, ye shall be free indeed."

John 8:36

Introduction

Many people live in fear because of curses, witchcraft, occult involvement, superstition, or past sins. The good news of the gospel is that Jesus Christ has power to set people free.

No matter what a person has done in the past, Christ offers forgiveness, healing, and a new beginning.

Jesus Came to Deliver

Throughout His ministry, Jesus brought freedom to those who were oppressed by evil.

He healed the sick, forgave sinners, cast out demons, and restored broken lives.

The Power of the Cross

When Jesus died on the cross, He defeated Satan and made salvation available to everyone who believes.

Because of Christ's sacrifice, believers can live in freedom rather than fear.

Breaking with the Past

Those who have participated in occult practices should completely turn away from them.

  • Confess your sins to God.
  • Ask Jesus for forgiveness.
  • Destroy occult objects and materials.
  • Commit your life fully to Christ.
  • Study God's Word daily.

A New Creation

The Bible says that anyone who is in Christ is a new creation.

Old habits, fears, and spiritual bondage do not have to control your future.

Living in Victory

Freedom is not only about leaving the past behind; it is also about walking daily with Jesus.

Prayer, Bible study, fellowship, and obedience help believers grow stronger in faith.

God's Protection

Believers do not need to live in fear of curses, witchcraft, or evil powers.

God promises His presence and protection to those who trust Him.

Hope for Everyone

No one is beyond God's grace. Christ can save, restore, and transform every person who comes to Him in faith.

Memory Verse

"If the Son therefore shall make you free, ye shall be free indeed."

John 8:36
