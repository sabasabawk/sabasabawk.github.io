👑 Lesson 4: Integrity and Character

Key Text

"The just man walketh in his integrity: his children are blessed after him."

Proverbs 20:7

Introduction

Leadership is built on character. A leader may possess knowledge, skills, and influence, but without integrity those qualities lose their value. God looks beyond outward appearance and examines the heart.

1. What Is Integrity?

Integrity means doing what is right even when no one is watching. It is living honestly, keeping promises, and remaining faithful to God's principles in every situation.

2. Character Is More Important Than Reputation

Reputation is what people think about us, but character is who we truly are. God desires leaders whose private lives match their public lives.

3. Daniel's Example

Daniel served faithfully in Babylon. Although his enemies searched for faults, they found none because Daniel was trustworthy and faithful to God.

His integrity brought honor to God and respect from kings.

4. Honesty Builds Trust

People willingly follow leaders they trust. Christian leaders must be truthful in speech, faithful in responsibilities, and fair in every decision.

5. Guard Your Heart

A leader's actions begin in the heart. Daily prayer, Bible study, and obedience to God strengthen godly character.

Lesson Summary

  • Integrity means doing what is right at all times.
  • Character matters more than reputation.
  • Daniel is a biblical example of integrity.
  • Honesty builds confidence and trust.
  • Godly character is developed through a close relationship with God.
