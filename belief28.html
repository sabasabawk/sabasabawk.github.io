Fundamental Belief #28

The New Earth

Key Bible Texts

Isaiah 65:17

Isaiah 66:22-23

2 Peter 3:13

Revelation 21:1-5

Revelation 22:1-5

What We Believe

On the new earth, in which righteousness dwells, God will provide an eternal home for the redeemed and a perfect environment for everlasting life, love, joy, and learning in His presence.

After sin and sinners have been destroyed, God will create a new heaven and a new earth. The effects of sin, suffering, pain, sickness, and death will never again exist.

The redeemed will live forever with God. They will enjoy direct fellowship with Him, worship Him throughout eternity, and experience unending peace, happiness, and fulfillment.

Life on the New Earth

  • No more death.
  • No more pain or suffering.
  • No more sickness.
  • No more tears.
  • Perfect peace and happiness.
  • Direct fellowship with God.
  • Eternal joy and worship.
  • A restored and beautiful creation.

Why It Matters

The promise of the New Earth gives believers hope and courage. It reminds us that God's plan of salvation will be completely fulfilled and that His children will enjoy eternal life in a world made perfect.

Practical Application

  • Keep your eyes on God's eternal promises.
  • Live faithfully while awaiting Christ's return.
  • Share the hope of eternal life with others.
  • Trust God's plan even during difficult times.
  • Prepare daily for eternity with Jesus.

Reflection Questions

1. What excites me most about the New Earth?

2. How does this promise strengthen my faith?

3. Am I helping others prepare for God's eternal kingdom?

Conclusion of the 28 Fundamental Beliefs

The 28 Fundamental Beliefs summarize the teachings of the Bible as understood by the Seventh-day Adventist Church. They point us to Jesus Christ, salvation, holy living, and the blessed hope of His soon return.

May these studies strengthen your faith, deepen your understanding of Scripture, and prepare you for God's eternal kingdom.
