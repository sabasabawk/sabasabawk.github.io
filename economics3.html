💰 Lesson 3: Biblical Stewardship

Key Text

"Moreover it is required in stewards, that a man be found faithful."

1 Corinthians 4:2

Introduction

Stewardship is one of the greatest responsibilities God has given His people. Since everything belongs to God, He calls us to faithfully manage what He has entrusted to us.

What Is Stewardship?

A steward is someone who manages another person's property. As Christians, we are God's stewards. Our responsibility is to use His gifts wisely and faithfully for His glory.

Areas of Stewardship

  • Our time
  • Our money
  • Our talents and abilities
  • Our health
  • Our possessions
  • Our influence
  • The environment God created

The Parable of the Talents

In Matthew 25:14–30, Jesus told the Parable of the Talents. The faithful servants wisely used what their master entrusted to them and were rewarded. The servant who buried his talent was rebuked because he failed to use what had been entrusted to him.

God expects us to use every blessing for His kingdom rather than wasting or neglecting it.

Characteristics of a Faithful Steward

  • Faithful
  • Honest
  • Responsible
  • Generous
  • Thankful
  • Willing to serve others

Blessings of Faithful Stewardship

Faithful stewardship brings peace, contentment, trust from God, and opportunities to bless others. Above all, it prepares us to hear the words: "Well done, thou good and faithful servant." (Matthew 25:21)

Application

Examine your life today. Are you managing God's gifts faithfully? Pray for wisdom to become a trustworthy steward in every area of your life.
