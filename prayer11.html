🙏 Lesson 11: Prayer and Revival

Key Text

"If my people, which are called by my name, shall humble themselves, and pray, and seek my face, and turn from their wicked ways; then will I hear from heaven, and will forgive their sin, and will heal their land."

2 Chronicles 7:14

Introduction

Throughout history, every great spiritual revival has been connected with earnest prayer. Revival is the renewal of spiritual life, a fresh experience with God that awakens faith, repentance, obedience, and love for Christ.

God desires His people to experience true revival, and prayer is one of the primary means through which revival comes.

What Is Revival?

Revival is a restoration of spiritual life. It occurs when believers draw near to God, repent of sin, and allow the Holy Spirit to work powerfully in their hearts.

Revival is not merely emotional excitement. True revival produces lasting change and deeper commitment to God.

The Role of Prayer in Revival

Prayer prepares the heart for the work of the Holy Spirit. As believers pray, they become aware of their need for God's grace and power.

  • Prayer leads to repentance.
  • Prayer strengthens faith.
  • Prayer deepens love for God.
  • Prayer unites believers.
  • Prayer opens the way for the Holy Spirit to work.

Biblical Examples of Revival

  • The revival under King Hezekiah.
  • The revival under King Josiah.
  • The outpouring of the Holy Spirit at Pentecost.
  • The spiritual awakenings described in the book of Acts.

Revival Begins Personally

Before a church or nation can experience revival, individual believers must seek God personally. Revival starts when we humble ourselves, confess our sins, and surrender our lives fully to Christ.

Every Christian should pray daily for personal revival.

Praying for Church Revival

Churches need revival today more than ever. Members should pray for unity, faithfulness, spiritual growth, and a renewed passion for evangelism.

A praying church becomes a powerful witness to the world.

Signs of True Revival

  • Greater love for God.
  • Increased Bible study.
  • More faithful prayer.
  • Repentance and confession.
  • Unity among believers.
  • Passion for sharing the gospel.

Memory Verse

"Wilt thou not revive us again: that thy people may rejoice in thee?"

Psalm 85:6
