🙏 Lesson 10: Prayer in Times of Trouble

Key Text

"Call upon me in the day of trouble: I will deliver thee, and thou shalt glorify me."

Psalm 50:15

Introduction

Every person experiences difficulties, disappointments, losses, and trials. During such times, prayer becomes a powerful source of strength and comfort. God does not promise a life free from trouble, but He promises to be with us through every storm.

Prayer allows us to place our burdens in God's hands and receive His peace and guidance.

God Is Our Refuge

The Bible repeatedly reminds us that God is our refuge and strength. When we feel overwhelmed, afraid, or discouraged, we can turn to Him in prayer.

"God is our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble."

Psalm 46:1

Biblical Examples

  • David prayed during times of danger.
  • Daniel prayed when threatened with death.
  • Jonah prayed from inside the great fish.
  • Paul and Silas prayed while in prison.
  • Jesus prayed in the Garden of Gethsemane.

What Prayer Does During Trials

  • Brings peace to troubled hearts.
  • Strengthens faith.
  • Provides courage.
  • Helps us trust God.
  • Guides us through difficult decisions.
  • Reminds us that we are never alone.

Trusting God's Timing

Sometimes God's answers do not come immediately. During such moments, prayer teaches patience and trust. God's timing is always perfect, even when we do not fully understand His plans.

Faith means trusting God even when the situation remains difficult.

Jesus in Gethsemane

Before His crucifixion, Jesus prayed earnestly in the Garden of Gethsemane. Although He faced great suffering, He submitted Himself completely to the Father's will.

His example teaches us to pray:

"Not my will, but thine, be done."

Victory Through Prayer

Prayer may not always remove our problems immediately, but it gives us strength to face them. Through prayer, God transforms fear into faith and weakness into courage.

Memory Verse

"Casting all your care upon him; for he careth for you."

1 Peter 5:7
