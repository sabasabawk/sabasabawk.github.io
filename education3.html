👨‍🏫 Lesson 3: God as the First Teacher

Key Text

"I am the Lord thy God which teacheth thee to profit, which leadeth thee by the way that thou shouldest go."

Isaiah 48:17

Introduction

The first teacher in history was God Himself. From the Garden of Eden, God instructed Adam and Eve, teaching them about life, work, obedience, relationships, and stewardship. Every true teacher follows God's example by leading others into truth.

1. God Taught Adam and Eve

God gave humanity knowledge before sin entered the world. He taught them how to care for creation, enjoy fellowship with Him, and obey His commandments.

2. God Continues to Teach His People

Throughout the Bible, God taught through prophets, priests, kings, judges, and ultimately through His Son, Jesus Christ.

3. Nature Is God's Classroom

God uses His creation to reveal His wisdom and power. Every flower, tree, mountain, and star points us to the Creator.

"The heavens declare the glory of God."

Psalm 19:1

4. The Holy Spirit Is Our Teacher

Jesus promised that the Holy Spirit would guide believers into all truth, helping them understand God's Word and apply it in daily life.

"He shall teach you all things."

John 14:26

5. Teachers Reflect God's Character

Christian teachers should be patient, loving, truthful, and compassionate, reflecting the character of the Great Teacher.

Lesson Summary

  • God is the first and greatest Teacher.
  • Education began in the Garden of Eden.
  • Nature reveals God's wisdom.
  • The Holy Spirit continues to teach believers.
  • Christian teachers should reflect God's character.
