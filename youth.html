Course Description

The Youth Ministry Course is designed to help young people build a strong relationship with Jesus Christ, develop Christian character, discover their purpose, and become effective witnesses for God.

This course contains 12 lessons and a certificate upon completion.

  • 📖 Lesson 5: Choosing Good Friends
  • 📖 Lesson 6: Overcoming Temptation
  • 📖 Lesson 7: Christian Character
  • 📖 Lesson 8: Using Talents for God
  • 📖 Lesson 9: Social Media and Faith
  • 📖 Lesson 10: Youth in Mission
  • 📖 Lesson 11: Leadership for Christ
  • 📖 Lesson 12: Preparing for Christ's Return
