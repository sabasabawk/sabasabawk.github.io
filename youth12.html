👥 Youth Lesson 12: Preparing for Christ's Return

Key Text

"And, behold, I come quickly; and my reward is with me, to give every man according as his work shall be."

Revelation 22:12

Introduction

One of the greatest promises in the Bible is the Second Coming of Jesus Christ. Christians eagerly await the day when Jesus will return to take His faithful people home.

This hope gives believers courage, purpose, and motivation to live faithfully each day.

The Promise of Christ's Return

Before Jesus ascended to heaven, angels assured the disciples that He would return again.

The Second Coming is not a symbol or a myth. It is a real event promised throughout Scripture.

Why Jesus Is Coming Back

  • To end sin and suffering.
  • To reward the faithful.
  • To establish His eternal kingdom.
  • To reunite believers with Him forever.

Living in Readiness

Since no one knows the exact day or hour of Christ's return, believers must always be spiritually prepared.

Preparation is not about fear but about maintaining a close relationship with Jesus.

How Youth Can Prepare

  • Pray daily.
  • Study the Bible.
  • Obey God's commandments.
  • Serve others faithfully.
  • Share the gospel.
  • Trust Jesus completely.

Keeping the Faith

The world offers many distractions, but Christians are called to remain faithful and focused on God's promises.

Young people who keep their eyes on Jesus can face the future with confidence.

The Blessed Hope

The Second Coming is called the "blessed hope" because it brings joy to believers.

One day Jesus will wipe away every tear, remove suffering, and establish everlasting peace.

A Call to Commitment

God invites every young person to surrender their life fully to Christ.

The best preparation for Christ's return is a daily relationship with Him.

Memory Verse

"And, behold, I come quickly; and my reward is with me."

Revelation 22:12
