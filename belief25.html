Fundamental Belief #25

The Second Coming of Christ

Key Bible Texts

John 14:1-3

Acts 1:9-11

Matthew 24:30-31

1 Thessalonians 4:16-17

Revelation 1:7

What We Believe

The second coming of Christ is the blessed hope of the church and the grand climax of the Gospel. Jesus Christ will personally, visibly, literally, and gloriously return to earth.

At His coming, the righteous dead will be resurrected and together with the righteous living will be caught up to meet the Lord in the air. The wicked living will be destroyed by the brightness of His coming.

Although no one knows the exact day or hour of Christ's return, Bible prophecy reveals signs that show His coming is near. Believers are called to live in readiness and faithfulness while sharing the Gospel with the world.

Signs of Christ's Return

  • Worldwide preaching of the Gospel.
  • Increase in natural disasters.
  • Wars and rumors of wars.
  • Growing moral decline.
  • Religious deception.
  • Global unrest and uncertainty.

Why It Matters

The Second Coming brings hope, comfort, and assurance. It reminds believers that sin, suffering, and death will not last forever. Jesus will return to take His faithful people home.

Practical Application

  • Live each day ready for Christ's return.
  • Study Bible prophecy.
  • Remain faithful in prayer and obedience.
  • Share the Gospel with others.
  • Trust God's promises.

Reflection Questions

1. Am I prepared for Christ's return?

2. How does the Second Coming give me hope?

3. What can I do to help others prepare for Jesus' return?
