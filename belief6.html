Fundamental Belief #6

Creation

Key Bible Texts

Genesis 1:1

Genesis 2:1-3

Exodus 20:8-11

Psalm 33:6-9

What We Believe

God is the Creator of all things and has revealed in Scripture the authentic account of His creative activity. In six literal days the Lord made the heavens, the earth, the sea, and everything in them, and rested on the seventh day.

Human beings were created in the image of God with individuality, freedom, and the ability to think and act. Although created free, humanity depends upon God for life, breath, and all things.

The biblical account of Creation establishes the foundation for the Sabbath, marriage, stewardship, and the value of every human life.

Why It Matters

Creation reminds us that we are not accidents but were intentionally created by a loving God. It gives meaning to life, reveals God's power and wisdom, and calls us to worship Him as our Creator and Sustainer.

Practical Application

  • Worship God as Creator.
  • Respect and care for God's creation.
  • Value every human being as created in God's image.
  • Observe the Sabbath as a memorial of Creation.
  • Live with gratitude for God's gifts.

Reflection Questions

1. What does it mean to be created in God's image?

2. How does Creation strengthen my faith in God?

3. How can I better care for God's creation?
