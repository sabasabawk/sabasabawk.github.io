📖 Lesson 1: What Is the Spirit of Prophecy?

Key Text

"And I fell at his feet to worship him. And he said unto me, See thou do it not: I am thy fellowservant, and of thy brethren that have the testimony of Jesus: worship God: for the testimony of Jesus is the spirit of prophecy."

Revelation 19:10

Lesson Study

The Spirit of Prophecy is God's special gift through which He communicates His will, guidance, warnings, and encouragement to His people through chosen messengers.

Throughout Bible history, God spoke through prophets such as Moses, Isaiah, Jeremiah, Daniel, and many others. These prophets delivered messages from God to guide His people.

The Seventh-day Adventist Church believes that the gift of prophecy continues to operate according to God's plan and that this gift was manifested in the ministry of Ellen G. White.

The Spirit of Prophecy never replaces the Bible. Instead, it points people back to Scripture and helps them understand and apply biblical principles.

The writings of Ellen G. White provide counsel on Christian living, education, health, family life, evangelism, and preparation for Christ's second coming.

Main Truths

  • God communicates through prophets.
  • The gift of prophecy is a biblical gift.
  • The Bible remains the supreme authority.
  • The Spirit of Prophecy points people to Jesus.
  • Ellen G. White exercised the prophetic gift in the Advent movement.
