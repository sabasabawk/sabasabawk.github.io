💰 Lesson 12: Biblical Principles for Financial Success

Key Text

"But seek ye first the kingdom of God, and his righteousness; and all these things shall be added unto you."

Matthew 6:33

Introduction

Throughout this course we have discovered that God cares about every area of our financial lives. Biblical financial success is not measured by how much money we possess, but by our faithfulness in managing what God has entrusted to us.

1. God Owns Everything

Everything belongs to God. We are His stewards, called to manage His resources wisely and faithfully.

"The earth is the Lord's, and the fulness thereof."

Psalm 24:1

2. Work Diligently

God blesses honest, hardworking people. Diligence, integrity, and perseverance lead to lasting success.

3. Manage Money Wisely

  • Create a budget.
  • Spend carefully.
  • Avoid unnecessary debt.
  • Save for future needs.
  • Plan before making financial decisions.

4. Honor God With Your Wealth

Faithfully return your tithe, give cheerful offerings, and use your resources to support God's work and help those in need.

5. Practice Honesty

God blesses those who deal honestly in business, employment, and every financial transaction.

6. Be Content

Contentment protects us from greed and materialism. True wealth is found in a close relationship with Christ.

7. Invest for Eternity

The greatest investment is leading people to Jesus and supporting His kingdom. Earthly riches fade away, but heavenly treasures last forever.

The Greatest Financial Principle

When God is first, every other area of life falls into its proper place. Financial success without God is temporary, but faithfulness to God brings blessings that last forever.

"Trust in the Lord with all thine heart... and he shall direct thy paths."

Proverbs 3:5–6
