📖 Lesson 11: Reading and Applying the Writings

Key Text

"Thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path."

Psalm 119:105

Lesson Study

The Spirit of Prophecy was given to help God's people grow spiritually and better understand biblical truth. The writings of Ellen G. White should be read prayerfully and thoughtfully.

As believers study these writings, they discover practical counsel for daily living, family life, health, education, evangelism, stewardship, and preparation for Christ's return.

The purpose of reading the Spirit of Prophecy is not merely to gain information but to experience transformation through the guidance of the Holy Spirit.

Every reader should compare all teachings with the Bible, which remains the supreme authority for faith and practice.

When the inspired counsels are applied in everyday life, they help develop Christlike character, strengthen faith, and prepare believers for service in God's kingdom.

How to Study the Spirit of Prophecy

  • Begin with prayer.
  • Study alongside the Bible.
  • Read regularly and thoughtfully.
  • Apply the principles to daily life.
  • Share what you learn with others.
  • Allow God's Spirit to transform your character.

Recommended Books

  • Steps to Christ
  • The Desire of Ages
  • The Great Controversy
  • Patriarchs and Prophets
  • Prophets and Kings
  • Acts of the Apostles
