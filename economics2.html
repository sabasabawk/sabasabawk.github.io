💰 Lesson 2: Work — God's Plan for Humanity

Key Text

"The Lord God took the man, and put him into the garden of Eden to dress it and to keep it."

Genesis 2:15

Introduction

Work is not a punishment—it is part of God's original design for humanity. Before sin entered the world, Adam was given meaningful work in the Garden of Eden.

Work Before Sin

God created Adam and gave him responsibility to care for the garden. This shows that work is holy and meaningful, not a curse.

Work After Sin

After sin, work became harder (Genesis 3:17–19), but it is still necessary and blessed when done for God's glory.

Biblical Principles of Work

  • Work diligently (Proverbs 10:4)
  • Work as unto God (Colossians 3:23)
  • Avoid laziness (Proverbs 6:6–11)
  • Be honest in all labor

Why Work Matters

Work provides for our needs, supports families, helps others, and glorifies God when done faithfully.

Application

Ask yourself: Am I working with honesty, discipline, and dedication as unto the Lord?

Memory Verse

"And whatsoever ye do, do it heartily, as to the Lord, and not unto men."

Colossians 3:23
