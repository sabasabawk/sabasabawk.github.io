📜 Lesson 12: The Continuing Relevance of the Spirit of Prophecy

Key Text

"Despise not prophesyings. Prove all things; hold fast that which is good."

1 Thessalonians 5:20-21

Lesson Study

The Spirit of Prophecy continues to be a blessing to the Seventh-day Adventist Church around the world. The counsels given through Ellen G. White remain relevant because they are based upon timeless biblical principles.

Her writings address challenges that Christians still face today, including spiritual growth, family life, education, health, evangelism, stewardship, and preparation for Christ's return.

Millions of believers continue to read her books because they strengthen faith, encourage obedience to God, and deepen understanding of Scripture.

The purpose of the Spirit of Prophecy is not to replace personal Bible study but to help believers draw closer to Jesus and live according to God's will.

As we approach the return of Christ, the messages of hope, faithfulness, and revival found in the Spirit of Prophecy remain as valuable as ever.

Key Lessons from the Course

  • The gift of prophecy is biblical.
  • God guided His church through Ellen G. White.
  • The Bible remains the supreme authority.
  • The Spirit of Prophecy points people to Jesus.
  • Christian character is essential.
  • Christ is coming again soon.
