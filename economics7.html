💰 Lesson 7: Giving, Tithes, and Offerings

Key Text

"Bring ye all the tithes into the storehouse, that there may be meat in mine house... and prove me now herewith, saith the Lord of hosts."

Malachi 3:10

Introduction

Everything we possess belongs to God. As faithful stewards, we honor Him by returning our tithes and giving freewill offerings. Giving is an act of worship that demonstrates our love, gratitude, and trust in God.

What Is Tithe?

A tithe is one-tenth (10%) of our increase or income. The Bible teaches that the tithe belongs to the Lord and should be returned faithfully.

Returning tithe is not merely giving a donation—it is returning to God what already belongs to Him.

What Are Offerings?

Offerings are voluntary gifts given in addition to the tithe. They support God's work, help those in need, and express thanksgiving for His blessings.

Why Should Christians Give?

  • To honor God.
  • To support the preaching of the gospel.
  • To develop an unselfish character.
  • To help those in need.
  • To express gratitude for God's blessings.

The Blessings of Faithful Giving

God promises to bless those who faithfully return their tithe and give cheerful offerings. These blessings are not limited to finances—they also include peace, joy, spiritual growth, and a deeper trust in God.

Giving Cheerfully

The apostle Paul wrote:

"God loveth a cheerful giver."

2 Corinthians 9:7

God values the attitude of our hearts more than the amount we give.

Application

Review your finances and prayerfully determine to return a faithful tithe and give regular offerings. Ask God to help you become a joyful and generous giver.
