💍 Marriage Lesson 5: Conflict Resolution

Key Text

"Be ye angry, and sin not: let not the sun go down upon your wrath."

Ephesians 4:26

Introduction

Conflict is a normal part of every marriage. Even loving couples sometimes disagree because each person has different experiences, opinions, and personalities.

The goal is not to avoid every conflict, but to resolve disagreements in a way that honors God and strengthens the relationship.

Understanding Conflict

Conflict itself is not a sin. Problems arise when disagreements are handled with anger, pride, harsh words, or selfishness.

Healthy conflict resolution can actually bring couples closer together by improving understanding and trust.

Common Causes of Conflict

  • Poor communication.
  • Financial disagreements.
  • Unmet expectations.
  • Parenting differences.
  • Lack of quality time together.
  • Stress and outside pressures.

Biblical Principles for Resolving Conflict

  • Pray before discussing the issue.
  • Speak the truth in love.
  • Listen carefully before responding.
  • Stay calm and respectful.
  • Seek solutions, not victory.
  • Forgive quickly.

Avoid Harmful Behaviors

Certain actions make conflicts worse and should be avoided:

  • Shouting and yelling.
  • Name-calling.
  • Threats and intimidation.
  • Bringing up past mistakes repeatedly.
  • Refusing to communicate.
  • Seeking revenge.

The Power of Forgiveness

Forgiveness is essential in marriage. Since both husband and wife make mistakes, both need grace and forgiveness.

Forgiveness does not mean ignoring problems. It means choosing to release bitterness and work toward healing.

Resolving Issues Quickly

The Bible advises believers not to let the sun go down on their anger. Couples should address conflicts as soon as reasonably possible rather than allowing resentment to grow.

Unresolved anger often creates larger problems later.

Christ Our Example

Jesus demonstrated patience, humility, mercy, and forgiveness. Christian couples should follow His example when dealing with disagreements.

A marriage that follows Christ's example can overcome even difficult challenges.

Memory Verse
