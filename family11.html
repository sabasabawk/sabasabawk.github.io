👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Lesson 11: Christian Leadership in the Home

Key Text

"But as for me and my house, we will serve the Lord."

Joshua 24:15

Lesson

A Christian home needs strong spiritual leadership grounded in God’s Word. Leadership in the family is not about control, but about service, guidance, and responsibility before God.

Parents are called to lead their families in prayer, Bible study, worship, and moral decision-making. A leader in the home reflects Christ’s humility and love.

Fathers and mothers should work together as a team, setting a godly example for children through their words and actions.

True Christian leadership builds unity, discipline, and spiritual direction in the home, helping each family member grow in Christ.

The Seventh-day Adventist understanding of family leadership emphasizes servant leadership modeled after Jesus Christ.

Principles of Christian Leadership

  • Lead the family in prayer and worship.
  • Be a good spiritual example.
  • Serve with humility, not control.
  • Make decisions based on God’s Word.
  • Work together as parents in unity.
  • Guide children in truth and love.
