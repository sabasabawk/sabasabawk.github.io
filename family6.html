💬 Lesson 6: Communication in the Home

Key Text

"Let your speech be always with grace, seasoned with salt..."

Colossians 4:6

Lesson

Good communication is the foundation of a strong and peaceful Christian home. Many family problems begin when people stop talking or speak without love and respect.

God designed the family to be a place of understanding, listening, and encouragement. Words have power—they can heal or hurt, build or destroy.

In a Christian home, family members should learn to listen patiently, speak kindly, and forgive quickly.

Parents should create an environment where children feel safe to express their feelings without fear. Children, in turn, should learn respect when speaking to parents.

The Seventh-day Adventist home is called to reflect Christ’s character through loving and respectful communication.

Principles of Good Communication

  • Listen before responding.
  • Speak with kindness and respect.
  • Avoid shouting and harsh words.
  • Be honest but loving.
  • Forgive quickly and avoid bitterness.
  • Pray before difficult conversations.

Memory Verse

"A soft answer turneth away wrath: but grievous words stir up anger."

Proverbs 15:1
