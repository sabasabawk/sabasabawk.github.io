👥 Youth Lesson 7: Christian Character

Key Text

"But the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, longsuffering, gentleness, goodness, faith."

Galatians 5:22

Introduction

Christian character is the reflection of Jesus Christ in our daily lives. It is not only about what we say but also about how we think, act, and treat others.

God desires every young person to develop a character that honors Him and positively influences others.

What Is Character?

Character is the collection of qualities that define who we are. It is revealed through our choices, habits, attitudes, and actions.

A strong Christian character is built on God's principles and guided by the Holy Spirit.

Jesus: Our Perfect Example

Jesus demonstrated perfect character. He showed love, humility, patience, honesty, forgiveness, and obedience to His Father.

As followers of Christ, we should seek to imitate His example.

Qualities of Christian Character

  • Love
  • Honesty
  • Kindness
  • Patience
  • Humility
  • Faithfulness
  • Self-control
  • Respect for others

Building Christian Character

Character is developed day by day through prayer, Bible study, obedience, and dependence upon God.

Every challenge and trial provides an opportunity for growth.

Character and Influence

People often notice our actions more than our words. A godly character can be a powerful witness for Christ.

Young people who demonstrate Christian character inspire others to follow Jesus.

Challenges to Character

  • Peer pressure
  • Dishonesty
  • Selfishness
  • Pride
  • Anger
  • Negative influences

Through God's grace, these challenges can be overcome.

The Fruit of the Spirit

The Holy Spirit produces Christlike qualities in our lives. As we remain connected to Jesus, His character becomes visible in us.

Character transformation is one of the greatest evidences of God's work in a person's life.

Memory Verse
