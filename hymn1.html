🎵 Hymn 001

Praise to the Lord

Theme: Worship & Praise

Scripture: Psalm 103:1-2

Author: Joachim Neander

Category: Opening Hymn

About This Hymn

This hymn is one of the best-known Christian hymns of praise. It calls believers to worship God as Creator, Sustainer, and Redeemer. It is frequently used during worship services and special occasions.

Bible Reflection

"Bless the Lord, O my soul: and all that is within me, bless his holy name."
