👥 Youth Lesson 6: Overcoming Temptation

Key Text

"There hath no temptation taken you but such as is common to man: but God is faithful, who will not suffer you to be tempted above that ye are able."

1 Corinthians 10:13

Introduction

Every Christian faces temptation. Temptation itself is not a sin, but giving in to temptation leads us away from God's will.

The good news is that God provides strength and a way of escape for those who trust Him.

What Is Temptation?

Temptation is the invitation to think, say, or do something that is contrary to God's Word.

Young people often face temptations involving peer pressure, dishonesty, harmful media, unhealthy relationships, and other challenges.

Jesus Faced Temptation

Jesus was tempted by Satan in the wilderness, yet He never sinned. He overcame temptation by trusting God and using Scripture.

His victory shows us that temptation can be resisted through God's power.

Common Temptations for Youth

  • Peer pressure.
  • Dishonesty.
  • Impure thoughts.
  • Harmful entertainment.
  • Disobedience to parents.
  • Neglecting prayer and Bible study.

How to Overcome Temptation

  • Pray regularly.
  • Study God's Word.
  • Avoid situations that encourage sin.
  • Choose godly friends.
  • Trust the Holy Spirit for strength.

The Power of God's Word

When Satan tempted Jesus, He answered with Scripture. Knowing Bible verses helps us recognize truth and resist evil.

The Word of God is a powerful weapon in spiritual battles.

God Provides a Way Out

According to 1 Corinthians 10:13, God always provides a way of escape. We are never alone when facing temptation.

God's grace is sufficient to help us remain faithful.

Victory Through Christ

We do not overcome temptation through our own strength. Victory comes through Jesus Christ.

As we depend on Him daily, He gives us the power to choose what is right.

Memory Verse

"God is faithful, who will not suffer you to be tempted above that ye are able."

1 Corinthians 10:13
