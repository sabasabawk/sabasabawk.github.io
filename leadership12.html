🏁 Lesson 12: Finishing Faithfully

Key Text

"I have fought a good fight, I have finished my course, I have kept the faith."

2 Timothy 4:7

Introduction

Starting well is important, but finishing faithfully is even more important. Throughout the Bible we find leaders who began well but failed to remain faithful, while others remained committed to God until the end. Christian leadership is a lifelong journey of obedience.

1. Remain Faithful to God

A Christian leader should never stop trusting, obeying, and serving God regardless of challenges.

2. Continue Growing

Great leaders never stop learning. Continue studying God's Word, praying, and developing your leadership skills.

3. Persevere Through Trials

Leadership often includes difficulties, criticism, and disappointment. God gives strength to those who remain faithful.

4. Finish Your Assignment

God has given every believer a purpose. Stay committed until the work He has entrusted to you is complete.

5. Leave a Godly Legacy

The greatest achievement is hearing Jesus say, "Well done, thou good and faithful servant." (Matthew 25:23)
