Lesson 4: The Sabbath

Key Text: Exodus 20:8-11

Introduction

The Sabbath is a special gift from God to humanity. It was established at Creation and set apart as a holy day of rest and worship. Through the Sabbath, God invites us to spend time with Him and remember that He is our Creator and Redeemer.

1. The Sabbath at Creation

Genesis 2:1-3 teaches that after creating the world in six days, God rested on the seventh day, blessed it, and made it holy.

2. The Sabbath in the Ten Commandments

Exodus 20:8-11 commands believers to remember the Sabbath day and keep it holy. This commandment reminds us that God is the Creator of heaven and earth.

3. Jesus and the Sabbath

Jesus honored the Sabbath and taught its true purpose. He said, "The Sabbath was made for man" (Mark 2:27).

4. Blessings of the Sabbath

The Sabbath brings spiritual renewal, physical rest, and a deeper relationship with God.

Reflection Questions

  1. Why did God create the Sabbath?
  2. How did Jesus honor the Sabbath?
  3. How can the Sabbath strengthen your faith?

Prayer

Heavenly Father, thank You for the gift of the Sabbath. Help me to honor this holy day and draw closer to You. In Jesus' name, Amen.
