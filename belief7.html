Fundamental Belief #7

The Nature of Humanity

Key Bible Texts

Genesis 1:26-27

Genesis 2:7

Romans 5:12

Psalm 8:4-8

What We Believe

Men and women were created in the image of God with individuality, the power and freedom to think and to act. Though created as free beings, each person is an indivisible unity of body, mind, and spirit, dependent upon God for life and breath.

When our first parents disobeyed God, they denied their dependence upon Him and fell from their high position under God. The image of God in humanity was marred, and they became subject to death.

Their descendants share this fallen nature and its consequences. They are born with weaknesses and tendencies toward evil. But God in Christ reconciled the world to Himself and restores in repentant people the image of their Maker.

Why It Matters

Understanding human nature helps us recognize our need for salvation. It reminds us that every person has great value because they were created by God, yet all need the grace and transforming power of Jesus Christ.

Practical Application

  • Treat every person with dignity and respect.
  • Recognize your dependence on God.
  • Seek Christ's power to overcome sin.
  • Care for your physical, mental, and spiritual health.
  • Live according to God's purpose for your life.

Reflection Questions

1. What does it mean that I was created in God's image?

2. How has sin affected humanity?

3. How is Christ restoring God's image in my life?
