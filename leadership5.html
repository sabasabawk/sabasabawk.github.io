🙏 Lesson 5: Prayer and Leadership

Key Text

"If any of you lack wisdom, let him ask of God, that giveth to all men liberally, and upbraideth not; and it shall be given him."

James 1:5

Introduction

Every leader faces important decisions. Human wisdom is limited, but God's wisdom is perfect. Prayer is the greatest source of strength and guidance for every Christian leader.

1. Jesus Prayed Often

Before choosing His disciples, performing great works, and facing the cross, Jesus spent time in prayer. He depended completely on His Father.

2. Prayer Brings Wisdom

Leaders are often required to solve problems and make difficult choices. God promises to give wisdom to those who sincerely ask Him.

3. Prayer Gives Strength

Leadership can be challenging. Prayer gives courage during trials, peace during difficulties, and strength to continue serving faithfully.

4. Pray for Those You Lead

Christian leaders should regularly pray for their families, churches, friends, coworkers, and communities. Interceding for others is an important part of leadership.

5. Stay Connected to God

Prayer is not only for emergencies. Daily communication with God keeps leaders humble, faithful, and spiritually strong.

Lesson Summary

  • Jesus made prayer a priority.
  • God gives wisdom to those who ask Him.
  • Prayer strengthens leaders during difficult times.
  • Christian leaders pray for the people they serve.
  • Daily prayer keeps leaders close to God.
