Fundamental Belief #23

Marriage and the Family

Key Bible Texts

Genesis 2:18-24

Exodus 20:12

Ephesians 5:21-33

Colossians 3:18-21

Joshua 24:15

What We Believe

Marriage was divinely established in Eden and affirmed by Jesus to be a lifelong union between a man and a woman in loving companionship. It was instituted by God for the happiness of humanity, the nurturing of children, and the stability of society.

The family is the primary setting where values are learned, faith is nurtured, and character is developed. Parents are called to raise their children in love, discipline, and instruction according to God's Word.

Family members are to respect, support, and care for one another. Through Christ, homes can become places of love, forgiveness, service, and spiritual growth.

God's Plan for Families

  • Love and mutual respect.
  • Faithfulness in marriage.
  • Prayer and worship in the home.
  • Teaching children God's truth.
  • Forgiveness and reconciliation.
  • Care for the needs of family members.
  • Building strong Christ-centered relationships.

Why It Matters

Strong families build strong churches and communities. God's plan for marriage and family provides security, love, guidance, and support for every generation. A Christ-centered home reflects God's love to the world.

Practical Application

  • Pray together as a family.
  • Study the Bible together.
  • Show love through actions and words.
  • Resolve conflicts with grace and forgiveness.
  • Spend quality time with family members.
  • Support and encourage one another.
  • Make Christ the center of your home.

Reflection Questions

1. How can my home better reflect God's love?

2. What can I do to strengthen family relationships?

3. How can Christ become more central in my family life?
