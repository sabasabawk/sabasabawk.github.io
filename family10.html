💑 Lesson 10: Christian Courtship

Key Text

"Can two walk together, except they be agreed?"

Amos 3:3

Lesson

Christian courtship is a period during which a man and a woman intentionally get to know one another while seeking God's guidance regarding marriage.

Unlike worldly dating that may focus mainly on emotions or physical attraction, Christian courtship places God at the center. Its purpose is to determine whether two people can build a Christ-centered marriage together.

A Christian should seek a partner who shares the same faith, values, and commitment to God. Spiritual compatibility is one of the strongest foundations for a successful marriage.

Courtship should be characterized by purity, honesty, respect, patience, and prayer. Couples should encourage one another spiritually and avoid behaviors that dishonor God.

The Seventh-day Adventist Church encourages young people to seek God's will carefully before making lifelong marriage commitments.

Principles of Christian Courtship

  • Put God first in the relationship.
  • Pray for wisdom and guidance.
  • Seek spiritual compatibility.
  • Practice purity and self-control.
  • Be honest and respectful.
  • Involve trusted parents, pastors, or mentors.
  • Prepare for a Christ-centered marriage.
