✝️ Lesson 10: Preparing the Family for Christ's Return

Key Text

"And if I go and prepare a place for you, I will come again, and receive you unto myself..."

John 14:3

Lesson

Every Christian family is living in preparation for the second coming of Jesus Christ. This is the blessed hope of all believers.

A family that prepares for Christ’s return focuses on spiritual growth, prayer, Bible study, and holy living.

Parents and children should encourage one another to remain faithful, avoiding worldly influences that can weaken faith.

Daily family worship, obedience to God’s commandments, and love for others are key signs of a family ready for Jesus’ return.

The Seventh-day Adventist message emphasizes readiness, endurance, and holiness as we await the soon coming of our Lord.

Principles of Preparation

  • Live a life of prayer and obedience.
  • Study the Bible daily as a family.
  • Keep faith in Jesus Christ.
  • Avoid sinful habits and worldly distractions.
  • Encourage one another spiritually.
  • Be ready for Christ’s second coming.

Memory Verse

"Be ye also ready: for in such an hour as ye think not the Son of man cometh."

Matthew 24:44
