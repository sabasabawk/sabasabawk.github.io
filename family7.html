⚖️ Lesson 7: Conflict Resolution in the Home

Key Text

"If it be possible, as much as lieth in you, live peaceably with all men."

Romans 12:18

Lesson

Every family experiences disagreements, but how those conflicts are handled determines whether the home becomes stronger or weaker.

God calls families to be peacemakers. Conflict should not be solved with anger, shouting, or silence, but with prayer, understanding, and forgiveness.

When conflict arises, family members should first calm down, then talk respectfully and listen to each other’s perspective.

Forgiveness is the key to healing relationships. Holding grudges destroys unity, but forgiveness restores peace.

The Seventh-day Adventist home should reflect Christ’s example of love, patience, and reconciliation.

Principles of Resolving Conflict

  • Pray before reacting.
  • Stay calm during disagreements.
  • Listen to understand, not to reply.
  • Avoid insults and blaming.
  • Seek forgiveness quickly.
  • Focus on solutions, not problems.

Memory Verse

"Blessed are the peacemakers: for they shall be called the children of God."

Matthew 5:9
