🙏 Prayer Course

Prayer is one of the greatest privileges given to God's people. Through prayer we communicate with our Heavenly Father, receive strength for daily living, and grow in our relationship with Jesus Christ.

This 12-lesson course will help you understand the purpose, power, and practice of prayer according to the Bible.

Lesson 11 – Prayer and Revival
Lesson 12 – The Power of a Praying Christian
