⭐ Lesson 7: Developing Character Through Education

Key Text

"Even a child is known by his doings, whether his work be pure, and whether it be right."

Proverbs 20:11

Introduction

One of the greatest goals of Seventh-day Adventist education is the development of Christlike character. Knowledge without good character can be dangerous, but education that shapes the heart prepares students to become faithful servants of God and responsible citizens.

1. Character Is More Valuable Than Wealth

Success is not measured only by education or possessions. God values honesty, integrity, humility, kindness, and faithfulness above worldly achievements.

2. Jesus Is Our Perfect Example

Jesus demonstrated love, obedience, compassion, patience, and self-control. Every Christian should seek to follow His example.

3. Good Habits Build Strong Character

Daily prayer, Bible study, honesty, punctuality, respect, diligence, and service to others help develop a strong Christian character.

4. Education Should Transform Lives

True education changes the way people think, speak, and live. It encourages students to make wise decisions based on biblical principles.

5. Character Prepares Us for Eternity

God desires to restore His image in every believer. Christian education helps prepare people to live faithfully now and to enjoy eternal life with Christ.

Lesson Summary

  • Character is more important than worldly success.
  • Jesus is the perfect model of character.
  • Good habits build strong character.
  • Education should transform lives.
  • Christlike character prepares us for eternity.
