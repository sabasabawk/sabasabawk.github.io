📜 Lesson 2: The Biblical Gift of Prophecy

Key Text

"And he gave some, apostles; and some, prophets; and some, evangelists; and some, pastors and teachers."

Ephesians 4:11

Lesson Study

The gift of prophecy is one of the spiritual gifts that God gives to His church. Throughout Scripture, God communicated His messages through prophets who were chosen to speak on His behalf.

Prophets did not speak their own ideas. They delivered messages that came from God through visions, dreams, inspiration, and direct revelation.

The Bible records many prophets including Moses, Samuel, Nathan, Isaiah, Jeremiah, Ezekiel, Daniel, John the Baptist, and others.

According to the Bible, the prophetic gift would continue in the Christian church. God promised to guide His people until the end of time through the gifts of the Holy Spirit.

A true prophet always points people to God, upholds Scripture, promotes obedience to God's commandments, and bears good spiritual fruit.

Tests of a True Prophet

  • The message agrees with the Bible.
  • The prophet exalts Jesus Christ.
  • The predictions are fulfilled.
  • The prophet bears good fruit.
  • The teachings lead people to God.
