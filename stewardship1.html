💰 Lesson 1: What Is Christian Stewardship?

Key Text

"The earth is the Lord's, and the fullness thereof; the world, and they that dwell therein."

Psalm 24:1

Introduction

Christian stewardship is the recognition that God is the Owner of everything and that we are His managers. Everything we possess—our lives, time, talents, money, health, and opportunities—comes from God.

A steward does not own the property he manages. Instead, he faithfully cares for it according to the wishes of the owner. In the same way, Christians are called to manage God's gifts wisely and faithfully.

God Owns Everything

The Bible teaches that God created the heavens, the earth, and all living things. Because He is the Creator, everything belongs to Him.

When we understand God's ownership, we become grateful and responsible. We recognize that our possessions are not merely ours but gifts entrusted to us by God.

Our Responsibility

God expects His stewards to:

  • Use time wisely.
  • Develop talents and abilities.
  • Care for their health.
  • Manage money faithfully.
  • Support God's mission.
  • Serve others with love.

The Faithful Steward

Jesus taught that faithful stewards are trustworthy, obedient, and prepared for His return. Faithfulness in small responsibilities prepares us for greater responsibilities in God's kingdom.

Memory Verse

"Moreover it is required in stewards, that a man be found faithful."

1 Corinthians 4:2
