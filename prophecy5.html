📖 Lesson 5: Daniel in the Lions' Den

Key Text

"My God hath sent his angel, and hath shut the lions' mouths, that they have not hurt me."

Daniel 6:22

Introduction

Daniel remained faithful to God throughout his life. Even when he became an old man serving under King Darius, his enemies tried to destroy him because of his faith.

This lesson teaches us about courage, prayer, and trusting God during difficult times.

Daniel's Excellent Spirit

King Darius appointed Daniel as one of the highest officials in the kingdom.

Daniel was honest, trustworthy, and faithful in all his responsibilities. Because of his excellent character, the king planned to place him over the entire kingdom.

The Plot Against Daniel

Other officials became jealous of Daniel and looked for a way to accuse him.

They could find no fault in his work, so they decided to attack him through his faith in God.

The Law of the Medes and Persians

The officials convinced King Darius to sign a law stating that for thirty days no one could pray to any god or man except the king.

Anyone who disobeyed would be thrown into a den of lions.

Daniel Continues to Pray

When Daniel learned about the law, he did not change his habits.

Three times a day he opened his window toward Jerusalem and prayed to God as he had always done.

Daniel chose obedience to God over obedience to an unjust human law.

Thrown Into the Lions' Den

The officials reported Daniel to the king.

Although King Darius respected Daniel, he could not change the law.

Daniel was thrown into the lions' den.

God Protects Daniel

The king spent a sleepless night worrying about Daniel.

Early the next morning he rushed to the lions' den and called out to him.

Daniel answered that God had sent His angel to shut the lions' mouths.

Daniel was completely unharmed.

Prophetic Significance

Daniel's experience points to future events when God's people will again face pressure to choose between obedience to God and obedience to human laws.

Like Daniel, God's faithful people must remain loyal to Him regardless of the consequences.

Lessons for Today

  • Prayer strengthens our relationship with God.
  • Faithfulness should not depend on circumstances.
  • God can protect His people in times of danger.
  • Obedience to God must come first.

Application

Develop a consistent prayer life like Daniel. Trust God even when doing what is right becomes difficult.
