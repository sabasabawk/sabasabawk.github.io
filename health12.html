🌟 Lesson 12: Healthy Living for God's Glory

Key Text

"Whether therefore ye eat, or drink, or whatsoever ye do, do all to the glory of God."

1 Corinthians 10:31

Lesson

Throughout this Health Course, we have learned that God cares deeply about our physical, mental, and spiritual well-being. Healthy living is not merely about avoiding disease—it is about honoring God with our entire lives.

When we follow God's principles of health, we can enjoy greater strength, clearer thinking, increased energy, and a better quality of life. Good health also enables us to serve God and others more effectively.

The Seventh-day Adventist Health Message encourages believers to practice healthy habits, trust in God's guidance, and remember that true health includes a relationship with Jesus Christ.

Our goal is not simply to live longer, but to live faithfully and bring glory to God in everything we do.
