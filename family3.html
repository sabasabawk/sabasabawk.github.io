❤️ Lesson 3: Roles of Husband and Wife

Key Text

"Submitting yourselves one to another in the fear of God."

Ephesians 5:21

Lesson

God designed marriage to be a loving partnership. Husbands and wives have different responsibilities, but both are equal in value before God.

The husband is called to love his wife as Christ loved the church. This means showing kindness, sacrifice, leadership through service, and spiritual guidance in the home.

The wife is called to support her husband, nurture the family, and work together with him in building a Christ-centered home.

Both husband and wife should respect one another, communicate openly, pray together, and seek God's wisdom in family decisions.

A happy Christian home is built on mutual love, respect, cooperation, and commitment to God's will.

Biblical Responsibilities

  • Love one another unconditionally.
  • Respect one another.
  • Pray together daily.
  • Support each other during challenges.
  • Raise children in the knowledge of God.
  • Work together for the good of the family.
