Lesson 1: Why Study Prophecy?

Many people wonder what the future holds. Wars, disasters, diseases, and uncertainty make people ask important questions:

  • What is happening in our world?
  • Does God know the future?
  • Is Jesus coming soon?
  • Can the Bible help us understand the last days?

The answer is yes. Bible prophecy reveals that God knows the end from the beginning and has shared His plans with His people.

What Is Prophecy?

Prophecy is a message from God that reveals future events. Through His prophets, God has shown what will happen in nations, churches, and the world before the return of Jesus Christ.

The books of Daniel and Revelation contain some of the most important prophecies in the Bible.

God Knows the Future

Unlike human beings, God knows everything. He sees the past, present, and future.

Because He loves us, He has revealed future events so that we may be prepared.

Key Verse:

"Surely the Lord God will do nothing, but he revealeth his secret unto his servants the prophets."
— Amos 3:7

Why Should Christians Study Prophecy?

  • To strengthen faith in God's Word.
  • To understand world events.
  • To prepare for Christ's return.
  • To avoid deception in the last days.
  • To gain hope and encouragement.

The Purpose of Daniel and Revelation

The books of Daniel and Revelation work together to reveal God's plan for humanity. They explain the rise and fall of kingdoms, the great controversy between good and evil, and the final victory of Jesus Christ.

Application

As you begin this course, ask God to give you wisdom and understanding. Prophecy is not given to create fear but to inspire faith and confidence in God's promises.

Reflection Questions

  1. What is prophecy?
  2. Why does God reveal future events?
  3. How can studying prophecy strengthen your faith?
  4. What do you hope to learn from this course?

Prayer

Heavenly Father, thank You for revealing Your plans through Bible prophecy. Help me understand Your Word and prepare my heart for the soon return of Jesus Christ. Amen.
