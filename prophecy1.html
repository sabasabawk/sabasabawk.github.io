📖 Lesson 1: Why Study Prophecy?

Key Text

"Surely the Lord God will do nothing, but he revealeth his secret unto his servants the prophets."

Amos 3:7

Introduction

Many people are curious about the future. They wonder what will happen to the world, whether Jesus will return soon, and how current events fit into God's plan.

Bible prophecy answers these questions. Through prophecy, God reveals important truths about the future so His people can be prepared.

What Is Prophecy?

Prophecy is a message from God that reveals future events or spiritual truths through His chosen messengers.

The books of Daniel and Revelation contain many prophecies that help us understand world history and the events leading to Christ's return.

God Knows the Future

Unlike humans, God sees the beginning and the end. Nothing surprises Him.

Because He loves us, He reveals future events so that we may trust Him and be ready for what is coming.

Why Study Prophecy?

  • It strengthens faith in God's Word.
  • It helps us understand world events.
  • It prepares us for Christ's return.
  • It protects us from deception.
  • It gives hope during difficult times.

Daniel and Revelation

The books of Daniel and Revelation work together. Daniel lays the foundation while Revelation expands the message.

Together they reveal God's plan for the world and the final victory of Jesus Christ.

Prophecy Builds Confidence

Every fulfilled prophecy proves that God's Word is trustworthy.

When we see prophecies fulfilled exactly as predicted, our faith in God becomes stronger.

Application

As you begin this course, pray for wisdom and understanding. Ask God to help you understand His prophetic Word and prepare your heart for Jesus' soon return.
