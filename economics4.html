💰 Lesson 4: Budgeting and Financial Planning

Key Text

"For which of you, intending to build a tower, sitteth not down first, and counteth the cost, whether he have sufficient to finish it?"

Luke 14:28

Introduction

God desires His children to be wise managers of the resources He has entrusted to them. One of the best ways to practice good stewardship is by preparing a budget and planning ahead before spending.

What Is a Budget?

A budget is a simple financial plan that shows:

  • Money received (Income)
  • Money spent (Expenses)
  • Money saved

A budget helps us live within our means and avoid unnecessary debt.

Why Should Christians Budget?

  • To avoid waste.
  • To prepare for emergencies.
  • To provide for our families.
  • To support God's work faithfully.
  • To reduce financial stress.

Example of a Simple Budget

Income Amount
Monthly Salary $500
Other Income $100
Total Income $600

Expenses Amount
Tithe & Offering $70
Food $180
Transport $80
Savings $100
Other Expenses $170

Planning Before Spending

Jesus taught the importance of planning before beginning a project. Wise planning prevents unnecessary debt and helps us accomplish our goals.

Biblical Principles

  • Spend wisely.
  • Avoid impulsive buying.
  • Save regularly.
  • Give faithfully to God.
  • Prepare for future needs.

Application

Make a personal monthly budget. Record your income, list your expenses, set aside savings, and remain faithful in returning your tithe and offerings.
