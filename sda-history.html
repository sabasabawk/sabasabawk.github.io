Lesson 1: The Great Awakening

The Great Awakening was a powerful religious revival that spread through America and other parts of the world during the 1700s and 1800s.

Many people began studying the Bible more seriously and became interested in prophecy and the Second Coming of Jesus Christ.

This revival prepared the way for the Advent Movement and later the Seventh-day Adventist Church.

Key Text: Matthew 24:14

Quiz: What was the Great Awakening?

Lesson 2: William Miller

William Miller was a farmer and Bible student who carefully studied the prophecies of Daniel and Revelation.

He concluded that Jesus would return soon and began preaching this message throughout America.

Thousands accepted his message and became known as Millerites.

Key Text: Daniel 8:14

Quiz: What books of the Bible did William Miller study closely?

Lesson 3: The Great Disappointment

Many believers expected Jesus to return on October 22, 1844.

When Jesus did not return as expected, believers experienced great disappointment.

However, continued Bible study led to a clearer understanding of Christ's ministry in heaven.

Key Text: Habakkuk 2:3

Quiz: In what year did the Great Disappointment occur?

Lesson 4: The Sabbath Truth

After the Great Disappointment, Advent believers continued studying the Bible.

They discovered that the seventh-day Sabbath was established by God at Creation and included in the Ten Commandments.

Many early Adventists accepted this truth and began keeping Saturday as the Biblical Sabbath.

Key Text: Exodus 20:8-11

Quiz: Which day is the Biblical Sabbath?

Lesson 6: Organization of the Church

As the Advent Movement grew, leaders saw the need for proper church organization.

In 1863, the Seventh-day Adventist Church was officially organized to coordinate evangelism, education, publishing, and mission work.

The new organization helped spread the gospel more effectively around the world.

Key Text: 1 Corinthians 14:40

Quiz: In what year was the Seventh-day Adventist Church officially organized?

Lesson 7: Adventist Beliefs

Seventh-day Adventists believe the Bible is God's inspired Word.

Major teachings include the Sabbath, the Second Coming of Christ, salvation through faith in Jesus, and the heavenly sanctuary.

These teachings are based on Scripture and point people to Jesus Christ.

Key Text: 2 Timothy 3:16

Quiz: What is the Adventist source of belief?

Lesson 8: Worldwide Mission

The Adventist Church spread rapidly through missionary work.

Missionaries established churches, schools, hospitals, and publishing ministries in many countries.

Today the Adventist message reaches millions of people worldwide.

Key Text: Matthew 28:19-20

Quiz: What helps spread the Adventist message worldwide?

Lesson 9: Adventist Church Today

Today the church serves millions of members worldwide through education, healthcare, and gospel outreach.
