👥 Youth Lesson 4: Studying the Bible Daily

Key Text

"Thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path."

Psalm 119:105

Introduction

The Bible is God's message to humanity. Through its pages, God reveals His character, His will, and His plan of salvation.

Young people who study the Bible daily gain wisdom, strength, and guidance for life's decisions.

Why Study the Bible?

  • To know God better.
  • To understand truth.
  • To strengthen faith.
  • To resist temptation.
  • To receive guidance for daily life.

The Bible Changes Lives

God's Word has the power to transform hearts and minds. As we read and apply its teachings, we become more like Jesus.

The Bible provides hope, comfort, correction, and encouragement.

Jesus and the Scriptures

Jesus frequently quoted Scripture during His ministry. When tempted by Satan, He responded with the words, "It is written."

This teaches us the importance of knowing God's Word.

How to Study the Bible

  • Begin with prayer.
  • Read carefully and slowly.
  • Ask questions about the text.
  • Compare verses with other verses.
  • Apply what you learn.

Developing a Daily Habit

Choose a specific time each day for Bible study. Many people find morning worship especially helpful because it prepares them for the day ahead.

Even a few minutes of Bible study each day can make a significant difference.

The Bible and Youth

Young people face many challenges and decisions. The Bible provides wisdom for friendships, education, relationships, career choices, and spiritual growth.

God's Word remains relevant in every generation.
