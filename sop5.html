✝️ Lesson 5: Christ-Centered Writings

Key Text

"And I, if I be lifted up from the earth, will draw all men unto me."

John 12:32

Lesson Study

One of the most important characteristics of the Spirit of Prophecy is that it exalts Jesus Christ. Ellen G. White consistently directed people to Jesus as their Savior, Redeemer, High Priest, and Coming King.

Her writings emphasize Christ's love, sacrifice, ministry, and second coming. Rather than drawing attention to herself, she pointed believers to a deeper relationship with Jesus.

Among her most beloved books is The Desire of Ages, which presents the life and ministry of Christ in a powerful and inspiring way. Many readers around the world have come to know Jesus more personally through this book.

The Spirit of Prophecy teaches that salvation comes through faith in Jesus Christ alone. His death on the cross provides forgiveness, hope, and eternal life for all who believe.

A true prophetic message always glorifies Christ and encourages believers to follow Him faithfully.

Major Christ-Centered Themes

  • Jesus is our Savior.
  • Jesus reveals God's love.
  • Jesus died for our sins.
  • Jesus ministers for us in heaven.
  • Jesus is coming again.
  • Every believer should have a personal relationship with Christ.
