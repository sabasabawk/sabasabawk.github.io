⚔️ Lesson 6: The Great Controversy Theme

Key Text

"And there was war in heaven: Michael and his angels fought against the dragon."

Revelation 12:7

Lesson Study

One of the most important themes in the writings of Ellen G. White is the Great Controversy between Christ and Satan. This conflict began in heaven when Lucifer rebelled against God.

After being cast out of heaven, Satan continued his rebellion on earth. Throughout history, the battle between good and evil has affected every person and every nation.

The Great Controversy theme helps us understand why suffering, sin, temptation, and death exist in the world. It also reveals God's plan to save humanity through Jesus Christ.

The book The Great Controversy traces this conflict from the destruction of Jerusalem through church history and into the final events before Christ's second coming.

Although Satan is a powerful enemy, Christ has already won the victory through His death and resurrection. In the end, sin will be destroyed and God's kingdom will be fully restored.

Main Lessons from the Great Controversy

  • God is a God of love and justice.
  • Satan is the originator of sin.
  • Every person must choose whom to follow.
  • Jesus provides victory over sin.
  • God's truth will triumph in the end.
  • Christ will soon return to establish His eternal kingdom.
