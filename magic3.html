🪄 Lesson 3: Simon the Sorcerer

Key Text

"Thy money perish with thee, because thou hast thought that the gift of God may be purchased with money."

Acts 8:20

Introduction

One of the most famous stories about magic in the New Testament is the account of Simon the Sorcerer. His story teaches an important lesson about the difference between God's power and human attempts to gain spiritual power.

Who Was Simon?

Simon lived in Samaria and practiced sorcery. Many people were amazed by his powers and considered him a great man.

Acts 8:9 says that he had bewitched the people of Samaria for a long time.

The Preaching of Philip

When Philip preached the gospel in Samaria, many people believed in Jesus and were baptized.

Simon himself believed and was baptized after witnessing the miracles performed through God's power.

Peter and John Arrive

Later, Peter and John came to Samaria and prayed for believers to receive the Holy Spirit.

Simon was amazed when he saw the power of God working through the apostles.

Simon's Mistake

Simon offered money to the apostles and asked them to give him the ability to bestow the Holy Spirit on others.

He viewed God's gift as something that could be bought and controlled.

Peter's Response

Peter strongly rebuked Simon and explained that God's gifts cannot be purchased.

Spiritual power comes from God alone and is given according to His will.

The Lesson for Christians

  • God's gifts cannot be bought.
  • True faith is not about power or fame.
  • The Holy Spirit is God's gift.
  • Spiritual blessings come through grace.
  • We must seek God with sincere hearts.

Magic Versus God's Power

Simon had spent years impressing people with sorcery, but he discovered that God's power was completely different.

God's power transforms lives and brings people closer to Him.

Repentance and Humility

After Peter's rebuke, Simon asked for prayer. His story reminds us that when we make mistakes, we should humble ourselves before God and seek His forgiveness.
