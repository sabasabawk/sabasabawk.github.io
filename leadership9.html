⏰ Lesson 9: Time Management for Christians

Key Text

"See then that ye walk circumspectly, not as fools, but as wise, redeeming the time, because the days are evil."

Ephesians 5:15–16

Introduction

Time is one of God's greatest gifts. Every person receives the same twenty-four hours each day. Wise Christian leaders manage their time carefully because they know every moment is an opportunity to glorify God.

1. Time Is a Gift from God

Each day is a blessing from the Lord. We should use our time to serve Him, care for others, work faithfully, and grow spiritually.

2. Set Godly Priorities

Begin each day with prayer and Bible study. Put God first, then give proper attention to family, work, ministry, and personal responsibilities.

3. Avoid Wasting Time

Laziness, unnecessary distractions, and poor planning can prevent us from fulfilling God's purpose. Wise leaders make every day count.

4. Plan Ahead

Successful leaders organize their responsibilities, prepare for important tasks, and use their time wisely instead of waiting until the last minute.

5. Balance Work and Rest

God established the Sabbath and the principle of rest. Christian leaders work diligently but also take time for worship, family, and physical renewal.

Lesson Summary

  • Time is a gift from God.
  • God should always be our first priority.
  • Wise leaders avoid wasting time.
  • Planning helps us become effective servants.
  • God values both faithful work and proper rest.
