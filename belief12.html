Fundamental Belief #12

The Church

Key Bible Texts

Matthew 16:18

Ephesians 2:19-22

1 Corinthians 12:12-14

Acts 2:42-47

What We Believe

The church is the community of believers who confess Jesus Christ as Lord and Savior. Called out from the world, they gather for worship, fellowship, instruction in God's Word, celebration of the Lord's Supper, service to humanity, and the worldwide proclamation of the Gospel.

The church derives its authority from Christ, who is the incarnate Word revealed in Scripture. The church is God's family on earth, united by faith, nourished by His Word, and empowered by the Holy Spirit.

As the body of Christ, the church exists to reflect His character and continue His mission of bringing salvation, hope, and truth to the world.

Why It Matters

The church provides spiritual support, encouragement, and opportunities for service. Believers grow stronger together through worship, prayer, Bible study, and fellowship. God designed the church to be a witness of His love and truth to the world.

Practical Application

  • Attend church faithfully.
  • Participate in worship and ministry.
  • Encourage and support fellow believers.
  • Use your talents to serve God's mission.
  • Help share the Gospel in your community.

Reflection Questions

1. What role do I play in the body of Christ?

2. How can I strengthen my local church?

3. How can I better support fellow believers?
