🙏 Lesson 6: Intercessory Prayer

Key Text

"I exhort therefore, that, first of all, supplications, prayers, intercessions, and giving of thanks, be made for all men."

1 Timothy 2:1

Introduction

Intercessory prayer is praying on behalf of others. Instead of focusing only on our own needs, we bring the needs, struggles, and concerns of other people before God.

Intercession reflects the love of Christ, who continually intercedes for His people before the Father.

What Is Intercessory Prayer?

Intercessory prayer means standing in the gap for someone else. It involves asking God to bless, guide, protect, heal, forgive, strengthen, or save others.

God invites His children to participate in His work by praying for family members, friends, church leaders, communities, and even enemies.

Biblical Examples

  • Abraham prayed for Sodom.
  • Moses interceded for Israel.
  • Samuel prayed for the people.
  • Job prayed for his friends.
  • Jesus prayed for His disciples.
  • Paul constantly prayed for the churches.

Jesus Our Greatest Intercessor

Jesus is the perfect example of intercessory prayer. Even while on the cross He prayed:

"Father, forgive them; for they know not what they do."

Luke 23:34

Today, Jesus continues to intercede for believers in heaven.

Who Should We Pray For?

  • Family members.
  • Friends and neighbors.
  • Church leaders.
  • Government leaders.
  • The sick and suffering.
  • Missionaries and evangelists.
  • Those who do not know Christ.
  • Those who have wronged us.

The Blessings of Intercessory Prayer

When we pray for others:

  • Our love for people grows.
  • Our faith becomes stronger.
  • We become more Christlike.
  • God works in powerful ways.
  • Lives can be transformed.

Memory Verse

"Pray one for another, that ye may be healed."

James 5:16
