💰 Lesson 8: Work and Diligence

Key Text

"And whatsoever ye do, do it heartily, as to the Lord, and not unto men."

Colossians 3:23

Introduction

Work is God's gift to humanity. Before sin entered the world, God placed Adam in the Garden of Eden to work and take care of it. Honest work glorifies God, develops character, and enables us to provide for ourselves and others.

God Values Hard Work

The Bible consistently encourages diligence and faithfulness. Those who work honestly reflect God's character and become a blessing to their families and communities.

The Danger of Laziness

The Scriptures warn against laziness because it leads to poverty, wasted opportunities, and spiritual weakness.

"He becometh poor that dealeth with a slack hand: but the hand of the diligent maketh rich."

Proverbs 10:4

Characteristics of a Diligent Worker

  • Works honestly.
  • Uses time wisely.
  • Is dependable.
  • Shows integrity.
  • Strives for excellence.
  • Serves others with humility.

Working as Service to God

Whether we are teachers, farmers, business owners, students, or employees, our work should honor God. Every task, no matter how small, can become an act of worship when done faithfully.

Rewards of Diligence

  • Financial stability.
  • Personal growth.
  • Respect from others.
  • Greater opportunities.
  • The joy of serving God through honest labor.

Application

Ask yourself: Am I giving my best in my studies, work, or business? Pray that God will help you become diligent, faithful, and trustworthy in everything you do.
