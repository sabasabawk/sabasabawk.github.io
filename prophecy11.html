📖 Lesson 11: The Seven Trumpets of Revelation

Key Text

"And the seven angels which had the seven trumpets prepared themselves to sound."

Revelation 8:6

Introduction

In Bible prophecy, trumpets often symbolize warnings, judgments, or important events.

The seven trumpets of Revelation reveal God's judgments throughout history and demonstrate that God remains in control of world events.

What Do Trumpets Represent?

In ancient Israel, trumpets were used to warn people, announce important events, and call armies to battle.

Similarly, the seven trumpets contain messages of warning for the world.

The First Trumpet

The first trumpet brought hail and fire mixed with blood upon the earth.

Many Bible students understand this as representing judgments that fell upon the Roman Empire.

The Second Trumpet

A great mountain burning with fire was cast into the sea.

This trumpet points to major upheavals affecting nations and kingdoms.

The Third Trumpet

A great star called Wormwood fell from heaven and affected rivers and fountains of waters.

This symbolizes bitter experiences and judgments upon parts of the world.

The Fourth Trumpet

The sun, moon, and stars were partially darkened.

This represents a period of spiritual and political decline.

The Fifth Trumpet

The fifth trumpet introduces the first woe.

John saw symbolic locusts emerging from the abyss, bringing distress and judgment.

The Sixth Trumpet

The sixth trumpet is called the second woe.

Powerful forces are released, bringing conflict and destruction upon the earth.

The Seventh Trumpet

The seventh trumpet announces the triumph of God:

"The kingdoms of this world are become the kingdoms of our Lord, and of his Christ."

This trumpet points to the final victory of Christ and the establishment of His everlasting kingdom.

The Message of the Trumpets

The trumpets remind us that God allows warnings before final judgment.

They reveal His mercy, justice, and desire for people to repent and turn to Him.

Lessons for Today

  • God controls the course of history.
  • His judgments are always fair.
  • Warnings are given because God loves humanity.
  • Christ's kingdom will ultimately triumph.

Application

Take God's warnings seriously. Draw closer to Jesus each day and trust Him as world events move toward the fulfillment of prophecy.
