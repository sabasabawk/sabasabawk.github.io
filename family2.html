💍 Lesson 2: Christian Marriage

Key Text

"Therefore shall a man leave his father and his mother, and shall cleave unto his wife: and they shall be one flesh."

Genesis 2:24

Lesson

Marriage is a sacred institution established by God. It is a lifelong covenant between a man and a woman based on love, faithfulness, commitment, and mutual respect.

Christian marriage reflects the relationship between Christ and His church. Husbands and wives are called to love, honor, support, and encourage one another.

A successful marriage is built upon prayer, trust, forgiveness, good communication, and obedience to God's Word.

No marriage is perfect, but when Christ is at the center of the home, couples can overcome challenges and grow stronger together.

The Seventh-day Adventist Church encourages couples to seek God's guidance and maintain strong spiritual lives together.

Principles of Christian Marriage

  • Love one another sincerely.
  • Practice forgiveness.
  • Communicate openly and honestly.
  • Pray together regularly.
  • Remain faithful to one another.
  • Keep Christ at the center of the relationship.

Memory Verse

"Above all these things put on charity, which is the bond of perfectness."

Colossians 3:14
