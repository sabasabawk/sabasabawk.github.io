🌿 Lesson 3: Nutrition and Balanced Diet

Key Text

"So whether you eat or drink or whatever you do, do it all for the glory of God."

1 Corinthians 10:31

Lesson

Good nutrition is one of the foundations of health. God has provided a variety of wholesome foods to nourish our bodies and help us maintain strength, energy, and wellness.

A balanced diet includes fruits, vegetables, whole grains, legumes, nuts, and adequate water. These foods provide essential vitamins, minerals, fiber, and nutrients needed for healthy living.

Poor nutrition can contribute to obesity, diabetes, heart disease, and many other health problems. Christians are called to care for their bodies because they are temples of the Holy Spirit.

The Adventist Health Message encourages simple, natural, and nutritious foods that promote physical, mental, and spiritual well-being.

Benefits of a Balanced Diet

  • Provides energy for daily activities.
  • Strengthens the immune system.
  • Supports healthy growth and development.
  • Reduces the risk of many diseases.
  • Improves mental and physical performance.
