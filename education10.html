🌍 Lesson 10: The Global Seventh-day Adventist Education System

Key Text

"Go ye therefore, and teach all nations."

Matthew 28:19

Introduction

Since its beginning, the Seventh-day Adventist Church has considered education to be one of its most important ministries. Around the world, Adventist schools, colleges, and universities help students grow academically, spiritually, physically, and socially while preparing them to serve God and humanity.

1. A Worldwide Mission

The Adventist education system serves millions of students across many countries. It exists to share the gospel while providing quality education based on biblical principles.

2. Education for Service

Adventist schools prepare students to become teachers, pastors, doctors, nurses, engineers, business leaders, and faithful Christians who positively influence society.

3. Academic Excellence with Christian Values

Students are encouraged to achieve high academic standards while developing honesty, integrity, compassion, and faith in Jesus Christ.

4. Learning Beyond the Classroom

Education includes community service, health education, leadership training, evangelism, music, agriculture, and practical work. Learning should prepare students for everyday life and Christian service.

5. Preparing Leaders for God's Kingdom

The ultimate goal of the Adventist education system is not only to prepare successful professionals but also faithful disciples who will help others know Christ and prepare for His second coming.

Lesson Summary

  • Adventist education is a worldwide ministry.
  • It combines academic excellence with biblical values.
  • Students are prepared for service and leadership.
  • Learning includes practical and spiritual development.
  • The highest goal is preparing people for God's kingdom.
