🙏 Lesson 3: The Lord's Prayer

Key Text

"After this manner therefore pray ye: Our Father which art in heaven, Hallowed be thy name."

Matthew 6:9

Introduction

One day the disciples asked Jesus, "Lord, teach us to pray." In response, Jesus gave them a model prayer known as the Lord's Prayer. This prayer is not merely to be repeated word for word; it is a guide that teaches the principles of effective prayer.

The Lord's Prayer

"Our Father which art in heaven,
Hallowed be thy name.
Thy kingdom come.
Thy will be done in earth, as it is in heaven.
Give us this day our daily bread.
And forgive us our debts, as we forgive our debtors.
And lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil:
For thine is the kingdom, and the power, and the glory, for ever. Amen."

Our Father in Heaven

Jesus teaches us to approach God as our loving Father. This reminds us that God cares for us and desires a close relationship with His children.

Hallowed Be Thy Name

Prayer begins with worship. We honor God's holy name and acknowledge His greatness, goodness, and power.

Thy Kingdom Come

Christians should pray for God's kingdom to be established and for Christ's soon return. We should desire God's rule in our hearts and in the world.

Give Us This Day Our Daily Bread

God invites us to bring our daily needs to Him. Whether physical, emotional, or spiritual, God is interested in every aspect of our lives.

Forgive Us Our Debts

Confession is an important part of prayer. We ask God for forgiveness and extend forgiveness to others just as He forgives us.

Lead Us Not into Temptation

We need God's protection and strength every day. Through prayer, God helps us resist temptation and overcome evil.
