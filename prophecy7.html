📖 Lesson 7: The Little Horn Power

Key Text

"And he shall speak great words against the most High, and shall wear out the saints of the most High."

Daniel 7:25

Introduction

In Daniel chapter 7, Daniel saw a mysterious "little horn" arise among the ten horns of the fourth beast.

God provided identifying characteristics so that this power could be recognized in history.

Review of Daniel 7

The four beasts represented:

  • Babylon
  • Medo-Persia
  • Greece
  • Rome

After Rome was divided, ten kingdoms arose. Among these divisions, the little horn appeared.

Characteristics of the Little Horn

The Bible gives several identifying marks:

  • It arose among the ten divisions of Rome.
  • It came after the ten kingdoms.
  • It uprooted three kingdoms.
  • It became different from the others.
  • It spoke great words against God.
  • It persecuted God's faithful people.
  • It attempted to change times and laws.
  • It exercised authority for a prophetic period.

A Religious-Political Power

The little horn was not merely a political kingdom.

It exercised both religious and political influence and became a major force in history.

Persecution of the Saints

Daniel saw that this power would oppose God's faithful people.

Throughout history many believers suffered persecution because they chose to follow Scripture above human traditions.

Attempting to Change God's Law

Daniel declared that the little horn would think to change times and laws.

This reminds us that God's law is eternal and cannot truly be altered by human authority.

The Heavenly Judgment

Although the little horn gained great influence, Daniel also saw that heaven would hold judgment.

God remains sovereign over history and will ultimately bring justice.

Christ's Everlasting Kingdom

The prophecy ends with hope.

The kingdoms of this world will pass away, but the kingdom of Jesus Christ will endure forever.

God's faithful people will share in His everlasting kingdom.

Lessons for Today

  • Truth must be tested by Scripture.
  • Human authority should never replace God's authority.
  • God knows the future.
  • Jesus will ultimately triumph over every earthly power.

Application

Study God's Word carefully and follow Christ faithfully. Place your trust in Jesus rather than human systems, knowing that His kingdom will never end.
