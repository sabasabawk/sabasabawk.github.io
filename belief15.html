Fundamental Belief #15

Baptism

Key Bible Texts

Matthew 28:19-20

Acts 2:38

Romans 6:3-6

Mark 16:16

What We Believe

By baptism we confess our faith in the death and resurrection of Jesus Christ and testify to our death to sin and our purpose to walk in newness of life. Through this act we acknowledge Christ as Lord and Savior and become members of His church.

Baptism is performed by immersion in water and follows repentance, faith in Jesus Christ, and instruction in the teachings of Scripture. It symbolizes cleansing from sin and the beginning of a new life in Christ.

As Jesus was baptized, believers follow His example as a public declaration of their commitment to God and His kingdom.

Why It Matters

Baptism is an outward expression of an inward transformation. It publicly demonstrates faith in Christ, commitment to His teachings, and membership in the family of God.

Practical Application

  • Accept Jesus Christ as your Savior.
  • Study and understand God's Word.
  • Prepare for baptism through prayer and repentance.
  • Live faithfully after baptism.
  • Encourage others to follow Christ.

Reflection Questions

1. What does baptism mean to me personally?

2. Have I fully committed my life to Christ?

3. How can I live out the promises represented by baptism?
