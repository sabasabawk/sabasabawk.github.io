Lesson 5: The Second Coming of Christ

Key Text: John 14:1-3

Introduction

The Second Coming of Jesus Christ is one of the greatest promises in the Bible. Jesus promised that He would return to take His faithful people home.

1. The Promise of His Return

Jesus said, "I will come again and receive you unto myself" (John 14:3).

2. Signs of the End Times

Matthew 24 describes signs that will occur before Christ returns, including wars, natural disasters, and the worldwide preaching of the Gospel.

3. How Jesus Will Return

The Bible teaches that Christ's return will be visible and glorious. Every eye shall see Him (Revelation 1:7).

4. The Resurrection of the Righteous

At Christ's return, the righteous dead will be raised and believers will meet the Lord in the air (1 Thessalonians 4:16-17).

5. Preparing for His Coming

Christians prepare by praying, studying the Bible, obeying God, and sharing the Gospel.

Reflection Questions

  1. Why is the Second Coming important?
  2. What signs point to Christ's return?
  3. How can you prepare for His coming?

Prayer

Lord Jesus, thank You for Your promise to return. Help me to remain faithful and ready for that glorious day. Amen.
