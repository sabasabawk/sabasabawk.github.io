Fundamental Belief #17

Spiritual Gifts and Ministries

Key Bible Texts

Romans 12:4-8

1 Corinthians 12:7-11

Ephesians 4:11-13

1 Peter 4:10-11

What We Believe

God bestows upon all members of His church spiritual gifts which each member is to employ in loving ministry for the common good of the church and humanity. These gifts are given by the Holy Spirit according to His will.

The gifts include ministries such as faith, healing, teaching, preaching, evangelism, leadership, encouragement, service, mercy, and many others. They are provided to equip believers for service and to build up the body of Christ.

When believers use their spiritual gifts faithfully, the church grows in unity, strength, and effectiveness in fulfilling Christ's mission to the world.

Why It Matters

Every believer has a role in God's work. Spiritual gifts help us serve others, strengthen the church, and share the Gospel. No gift is unimportant, and every member is valuable in God's plan.

Examples of Spiritual Gifts

  • Teaching
  • Preaching
  • Evangelism
  • Leadership
  • Encouragement
  • Service
  • Mercy
  • Faith
  • Giving
  • Hospitality

Practical Application

  • Discover your spiritual gifts.
  • Use your talents in ministry.
  • Support the work of the church.
  • Serve others with love and humility.
  • Allow the Holy Spirit to guide your service.

Reflection Questions

1. What gifts has God given me?

2. How am I using my gifts to serve others?

3. What ministry can I become involved in today?
