Abba

A loving name for God meaning "Father." Romans 8:15.

Altar

A place of worship and sacrifice.

Amen

Meaning "so be it" or "truly."

Angel

A heavenly messenger sent by God.

Anointing

Being set apart for God's service.

Apostle

One sent to preach the gospel.

Atonement

Reconciliation between God and humanity through Christ.

Baptism

A public declaration of faith in Jesus.

Blessing

God's favor and goodness upon people.

Blood of Christ

The sacrifice of Jesus for the forgiveness of sins.

Canticle

A biblical song of praise.

Covenant

A sacred agreement between God and His people.

Creation

God's work of making the universe.

Cross

The instrument on which Jesus was crucified.

David

The shepherd king of Israel.

Disciple

A follower of Jesus Christ.

Eden

The garden where Adam and Eve lived.

Elder

A church leader.

Eternal Life

Everlasting life through Jesus Christ.

Evangelism

Sharing the gospel with others.

Faith

Trust and confidence in God.

Fasting

Abstaining from food for spiritual purposes.

Forgiveness

God's removal of sin and guilt.

Gentiles

Non-Jewish peoples.

Glory

The majesty and splendor of God.

Gospel

The good news of salvation through Jesus.

Grace

God's unmerited favor.

Hallelujah

Meaning "Praise the Lord."

Heaven

The dwelling place of God.

Holiness

Being set apart for God.

Holy Spirit

The third Person of the Godhead.

Hope

Confident expectation in God's promises.

Idolatry

Worshiping anything instead of God.

Immanuel

Meaning "God with us."

Israel

God's covenant nation.

Jerusalem

The holy city of Israel.

Jesus Christ

The Son of God and Savior of the world.

Justification

Being declared righteous by faith.

Kingdom of God

God's rule and reign.

Lamb of God

A title for Jesus Christ.

Law

God's commandments and instructions.

Light

A symbol of truth and righteousness.

Love

The greatest Christian virtue.

Messiah

The Anointed One, Jesus Christ.

Mercy

God's compassion toward sinners.

Miracle

A supernatural act of God.

Mission

Sharing God's message with the world.

Moses

The leader who led Israel out of Egypt.

New Covenant

God's covenant through Jesus Christ.

Noah

The builder of the ark.
