📖 Bible Dictionary

Faith

Trust and confidence in God and His promises. Hebrews 11:1.

Grace

God's unmerited favor toward sinners through Jesus Christ. Ephesians 2:8.

Sabbath

The seventh day of the week set apart by God for rest and worship. Genesis 2:1-3; Exodus 20:8-11.

Sanctuary

The dwelling place of God and the center of His plan of salvation. Exodus 25:8.

Prophecy

A message inspired by God revealing His will and future events. 2 Peter 1:21.

Salvation

Deliverance from sin through faith in Jesus Christ. Acts 4:12.

Redemption

Christ's work of purchasing humanity back from sin through His sacrifice.

Justification

Being declared righteous before God through faith in Christ.

Discipleship

Following Jesus, learning from Him, and obeying His teachings.

Second Coming

The glorious return of Jesus Christ to take His people home.
