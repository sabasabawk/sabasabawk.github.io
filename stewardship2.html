⏰ Lesson 2: Stewardship of Time

Key Text

"See then that ye walk circumspectly, not as fools, but as wise, redeeming the time, because the days are evil."

Ephesians 5:15-16

Introduction

Time is one of God's greatest gifts. Every person receives the same twenty-four hours each day. Once time is gone, it can never be recovered. Therefore, God calls His children to use time wisely and purposefully.

Time Belongs to God

Just as our money, talents, and possessions belong to God, our time also belongs to Him. We are stewards of every hour, minute, and second that God gives us.

Faithful Christians seek to honor God through how they spend their days. They make time for worship, prayer, Bible study, family, work, and service.

Jesus as Our Example

Jesus lived a balanced and purposeful life. He spent time in prayer, teaching, healing, and helping others. He never wasted time, yet He always had time for people in need.

Wise Use of Time

  • Begin each day with prayer.
  • Study God's Word daily.
  • Avoid habits that waste time.
  • Be faithful in work and responsibilities.
  • Make time for family and service.
  • Prepare for Christ's return.

The Sabbath and Time Stewardship

The Sabbath teaches us that God deserves a special portion of our time. Each week we dedicate sacred hours to worship, rest, and fellowship with Him.

Memory Verse

Psalm 90:12
