🌿 Lesson 8: Temperance and Self-Control

Key Text

"And every man that striveth for the mastery is temperate in all things."

1 Corinthians 9:25

Lesson

Temperance means practicing moderation in good things and completely avoiding things that are harmful. It is one of the important principles of the Adventist Health Message.

God desires His people to develop self-control in eating, drinking, speaking, working, and every area of life. Self-control helps us make wise decisions that honor God.

The Bible warns against harmful substances such as alcohol, tobacco, and drugs because they damage the body and mind. Since our bodies are temples of the Holy Spirit, we should avoid anything that weakens our health or spiritual life.

Through the power of the Holy Spirit, Christians can develop self-discipline and live balanced lives that glorify God.

Benefits of Temperance

  • Protects physical health.
  • Improves mental clarity.
  • Strengthens self-discipline.
  • Helps resist harmful habits.
  • Honors God through healthy living.

Memory Verse

Philippians 4:13
