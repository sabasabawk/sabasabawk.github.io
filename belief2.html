Fundamental Belief #2

The Trinity

Key Bible Texts

Matthew 28:19

2 Corinthians 13:14

Deuteronomy 6:4

What We Believe

There is one God: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, a unity of three co-eternal Persons. God is immortal, all-powerful, all-knowing, above all, and ever present. He is infinite and beyond human understanding, yet known through His self-revelation.

The Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit work together in perfect harmony in the creation of the world, the plan of salvation, and the guidance of believers. Although distinct Persons, they are united in purpose, character, and love.

Why It Matters

The doctrine of the Trinity helps us understand God's love and His work for humanity. The Father planned salvation, the Son accomplished salvation through His life, death, and resurrection, and the Holy Spirit applies salvation to believers.

Practical Application

  • Worship God with reverence and faith.
  • Pray in the name of Jesus Christ.
  • Allow the Holy Spirit to guide your life.
  • Trust God's wisdom and power.
  • Reflect God's love in your relationships.

Reflection Questions

1. What does the Trinity teach me about God's love?

2. How do I experience the work of the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit in my life?

3. How can I grow closer to God each day?
