💰 Lesson 11: Wealth and Eternal Investment

Key Text

"Lay not up for yourselves treasures upon earth... But lay up for yourselves treasures in heaven."

Matthew 6:19–20

Introduction

God blesses His people with resources so they can glorify Him and bless others. While earthly wealth has value, Jesus taught that eternal treasures are far more important than temporary possessions.

What Is Eternal Investment?

Eternal investment means using our money, talents, time, and possessions to advance God's kingdom. Every act of generosity, service, and faithfulness has eternal value.

Earthly Wealth Is Temporary

Money, houses, and possessions will not last forever. They can be lost, stolen, or destroyed. Therefore, Christians should never place their ultimate hope in material wealth.

"For where your treasure is, there will your heart be also."

Matthew 6:21

Ways to Invest in God's Kingdom

  • Support gospel ministry.
  • Help the poor and needy.
  • Return faithful tithes and offerings.
  • Share the gospel with others.
  • Use your talents to serve God's church.
  • Encourage and disciple other believers.

The Rich Fool

Jesus told the parable of the rich fool who stored up great wealth but ignored God. That very night his life ended, showing that earthly riches cannot save anyone.

True success is being "rich toward God."

God Rewards Faithfulness

God notices every sacrifice made for His kingdom. Even small acts of generosity done with love are precious in His sight and will receive an eternal reward.

Application

Think about how you use your money and resources. Are they helping only yourself, or are they also advancing God's work? Ask God to help you invest in what lasts forever.
