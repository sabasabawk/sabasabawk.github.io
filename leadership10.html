🤝 Lesson 10: Teamwork and Unity

Key Text

"Endeavouring to keep the unity of the Spirit in the bond of peace."

Ephesians 4:3

Introduction

God never intended His work to be done by one person alone. Throughout the Bible, God's people worked together to accomplish His mission. Strong leaders build strong teams that are united in Christ.

1. We Are One Body in Christ

Every believer has different gifts, but all are important. Just as the human body has many parts, the church functions best when everyone serves faithfully.

"For as the body is one, and hath many members... so also is Christ."

1 Corinthians 12:12

2. Respect Every Team Member

Christian leaders value every person regardless of age, education, position, or background. Every member contributes to God's work.

3. Encourage Cooperation

Leaders should inspire people to work together instead of competing against one another. Unity strengthens the mission of the church.

4. Resolve Differences with Love

Disagreements should be handled with humility, forgiveness, and prayer. Unity does not mean everyone is identical—it means everyone is committed to Christ.

5. Keep the Mission First

When everyone focuses on winning souls and glorifying God, personal differences become less important than God's purpose.

Lesson Summary

  • God blesses teamwork.
  • Every believer has an important role.
  • Unity strengthens God's work.
  • Leaders encourage cooperation.
  • Christ is the center of every successful team.
