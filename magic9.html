📖 Lesson 9: Testing Spiritual Claims

Key Text

"Beloved, believe not every spirit, but try the spirits whether they are of God."

1 John 4:1

Introduction

Not every spiritual experience, vision, miracle, dream, or teaching comes from God. The Bible warns believers to test spiritual claims carefully.

Many false teachings appear convincing because they contain some truth mixed with error.

Why Testing Is Necessary

Jesus warned that false prophets and false teachers would arise and attempt to deceive many people.

Because of this danger, Christians must examine every teaching by the Word of God.

The Bible Is the Standard

God's Word is the final authority for faith and practice.

Any teaching that contradicts Scripture should be rejected regardless of how impressive it appears.

Signs and Wonders

Miracles alone do not prove that a message comes from God.

The Bible teaches that false prophets may perform signs and wonders to deceive people.

Truth must always be tested by Scripture.

Questions to Ask

  • Does this teaching agree with the Bible?
  • Does it honor Jesus Christ?
  • Does it encourage obedience to God?
  • Does it produce good spiritual fruit?
  • Is it consistent with biblical truth?

The Example of the Bereans

Acts 17:11 praises the Bereans because they examined the Scriptures daily to verify what they were being taught.

They did not blindly accept teachings without investigation.

The Holy Spirit and Truth

The Holy Spirit never contradicts the Bible because He inspired the Scriptures.

True spiritual guidance will always agree with God's Word.

Living Wisely

Christians should be humble, prayerful, and careful when evaluating spiritual claims.

God promises wisdom to those who sincerely seek Him.

Memory Verse

1 John 4:1
