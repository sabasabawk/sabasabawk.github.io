💰 Lesson 5: Tithes and Offerings

Key Text

"Bring ye all the tithes into the storehouse, that there may be meat in mine house."

Malachi 3:10

Introduction

Tithes and offerings are important expressions of worship and faithfulness. Through them, God's people acknowledge that He is the source of all blessings and demonstrate trust in His provision.

What Is Tithe?

A tithe is one-tenth (10%) of a person's increase or income. The Bible teaches that the tithe belongs to God and should be returned faithfully.

Abraham returned tithe to God before the nation of Israel existed, showing that tithing is a biblical principle that predates the ceremonial laws.

Purpose of Tithe

The tithe supports the preaching of the gospel and the ministry of God's church. It helps provide for those who dedicate their lives to sharing God's message.

  • Supports gospel ministry.
  • Advances mission work.
  • Demonstrates faithfulness.
  • Recognizes God's ownership.

What Are Offerings?

Offerings are gifts given freely in addition to the tithe. While tithe is a fixed portion, offerings are voluntary expressions of gratitude, love, and worship.

God loves a cheerful giver and blesses those who give willingly and generously.

Blessings of Faithfulness

God promises spiritual blessings to those who trust Him with their resources. Faithful giving develops faith, gratitude, and dependence upon God.

Returning tithe and giving offerings are acts of worship that honor God and help spread the gospel throughout the world.
