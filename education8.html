📖 Lesson 8: Faith and Learning Together

Key Text

"Trust in the Lord with all thine heart; and lean not unto thine own understanding."

Proverbs 3:5

Introduction

In Seventh-day Adventist education, faith and learning cannot be separated. Every subject is taught from a biblical worldview, recognizing God as the Creator and Sustainer of all knowledge. True education helps students grow both intellectually and spiritually.

1. God Is the Source of Truth

All genuine truth comes from God. Science, history, mathematics, language, and every field of study reveal His wisdom when understood correctly.

2. Learning Strengthens Faith

Education should deepen our trust in God. As students learn about the world, they should also recognize God's power, order, and love in creation.

3. Faith Guides Decision-Making

Biblical principles help students make wise choices in school, at home, at work, and in society. Knowledge without faith can easily be misused.

4. Excellence Honors God

Students should strive for excellence, not merely for personal success, but to glorify God with the abilities He has given them.

5. Learning Never Ends

Christian education encourages a lifelong love of learning. As followers of Christ, we continue growing in wisdom, understanding, and faith throughout our lives.

Lesson Summary

  • Faith and learning belong together.
  • God is the source of all truth.
  • Education should strengthen faith.
  • Excellence brings glory to God.
  • Learning continues throughout life.
