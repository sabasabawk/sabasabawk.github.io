💰 Lesson 8: Financial Stewardship

Key Text

"Moreover it is required in stewards, that a man be found faithful."

1 Corinthians 4:2

Lesson

Everything we possess ultimately belongs to God. He has entrusted us with time, talents, resources, and finances to manage wisely for His glory.

Financial stewardship means using money responsibly and according to biblical principles. Christian families should plan carefully, avoid unnecessary debt, save wisely, and spend responsibly.

The Bible teaches the importance of returning faithful tithes and offerings, helping those in need, and supporting God's work.

Good financial management reduces stress within the family and helps create stability and peace in the home.

When families honor God with their finances, they demonstrate trust in His provision and guidance.

Principles of Financial Stewardship

  • Remember that God owns everything.
  • Return faithful tithes and offerings.
  • Create a family budget.
  • Save for future needs.
  • Avoid unnecessary debt.
  • Help those in need.
  • Use resources wisely.

Memory Verse

Proverbs 3:9
