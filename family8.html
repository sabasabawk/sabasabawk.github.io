💍 Lesson 8: Love and Respect in Marriage

Key Text

"Husbands, love your wives, even as Christ also loved the church..."

Ephesians 5:25

Lesson

Marriage is a sacred covenant established by God. It is built on love, respect, trust, and commitment between husband and wife.

In a Christian home, the husband is called to love his wife sacrificially, just as Christ loves the church. The wife is called to respect and support her husband with kindness and wisdom.

True love in marriage is not based on feelings alone but on daily choices of patience, forgiveness, and service to one another.

When Christ is the center of marriage, the home becomes a place of peace, unity, and spiritual growth.

The Seventh-day Adventist understanding of marriage emphasizes faithfulness, mutual respect, and lifelong commitment under God.

Principles of Christian Marriage

  • Put God first in marriage.
  • Love sacrificially and unconditionally.
  • Show mutual respect.
  • Communicate openly and honestly.
  • Forgive each other quickly.
  • Remain faithful and committed.

Memory Verse

Ephesians 5:33
