💰 Lesson 1: God's Ownership of Everything

Key Text

"The earth is the Lord's, and the fulness thereof; the world, and they that dwell therein."

Psalm 24:1

Introduction

Many people believe that what they own belongs entirely to them. The Bible teaches something different. Everything belongs to God because He is the Creator of heaven, earth, and all living things.

Our money, talents, time, possessions, careers, businesses, and even our lives are gifts from Him. We are not owners—we are stewards entrusted with God's resources.

God Is the Creator

Genesis 1:1 declares, "In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth." Because God created everything, He has ownership over everything.

Nothing we possess originated from ourselves. Every blessing ultimately comes from God.

We Are Stewards

A steward is someone who manages property belonging to another person.

God has entrusted us with finances, families, health, education, opportunities, and spiritual gifts. One day we will give an account of how we managed His blessings.

Why This Truth Matters

  • It teaches humility.
  • It reminds us to depend upon God.
  • It encourages generosity.
  • It helps us avoid greed.
  • It teaches faithful stewardship.

Biblical Examples

Abraham recognized that everything came from God. Job declared, "The Lord gave, and the Lord hath taken away; blessed be the name of the Lord." (Job 1:21) King David also prayed that both riches and honor come from God (1 Chronicles 29:12).

Application

Ask yourself:

  • Do I recognize God as the Owner of my possessions?
  • Am I managing my money in a way that honors Him?
  • Do I use my talents for God's glory?

Faithful stewardship begins when we acknowledge God's ownership.
