Fundamental Belief #1

The Holy Scriptures

Key Bible Texts

2 Timothy 3:16-17

Psalm 119:105

2 Peter 1:20-21

What We Believe

The Holy Scriptures, Old and New Testaments, are the written Word of God. Holy men of God spoke and wrote as they were moved by the Holy Spirit. In His Word, God has committed to humanity the knowledge necessary for salvation.

The Bible is the supreme and authoritative revelation of God's will, character, and plan of redemption. It is the standard of character, the test of experience, the authoritative revealer of doctrines, and the trustworthy record of God's acts in history.

Why It Matters

The Bible helps us know Jesus Christ, understand salvation, and learn how to live according to God's will. Through daily Bible study we grow spiritually and strengthen our faith.

Practical Application

  • Read the Bible every day.
  • Pray before studying Scripture.
  • Follow biblical teachings.
  • Share God's Word with others.
  • Memorize important verses.

Reflection Questions

1. How often do I study the Bible?

2. What is God teaching me through His Word?

3. How can I apply Scripture today?
