Fundamental Belief #8

The Great Controversy

Key Bible Texts

Revelation 12:7-9

Genesis 3:15

John 16:33

Ephesians 6:11-12

What We Believe

All humanity is now involved in a great controversy between Christ and Satan concerning the character of God, His law, and His sovereignty over the universe. This conflict began in heaven when Lucifer, a created being, became self-exalted and led a rebellion against God.

Cast out of heaven, Satan brought the spirit of rebellion to this world. By leading Adam and Eve into sin, he caused humanity to fall. As a result, the world experiences suffering, pain, injustice, and death.

Through His life, death, resurrection, and heavenly ministry, Jesus Christ has defeated Satan and secured victory for all who trust in Him. The Great Controversy will end when sin and sinners are destroyed and God's love, justice, and righteousness are fully revealed throughout the universe.

Why It Matters

The Great Controversy helps explain why evil exists in a world created by a loving God. It reminds us that we are not fighting alone and that Christ has already won the ultimate victory over sin and Satan.

Practical Application

  • Trust Jesus during trials and temptations.
  • Study God's Word daily.
  • Put on the whole armor of God.
  • Choose obedience to God over sin.
  • Share the hope of Christ's victory with others.

Reflection Questions

1. How do I see the Great Controversy affecting the world today?

2. How can I stand firmly on Christ's side?

3. What gives me confidence in Christ's final victory?
