🎓 Lesson 1: What Is Seventh-day Adventist Education?

Key Text

"The fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom: and the knowledge of the holy is understanding."

Proverbs 9:10

Introduction

Education is more than earning certificates or preparing for a career. According to the Bible, true education prepares people to know God, develop Christlike character, serve others, and live wisely. Seventh-day Adventist education is based on biblical principles and seeks the complete development of the physical, mental, social, and spiritual life.

1. God Is the Source of All Wisdom

The Bible teaches that all true wisdom comes from God. Human knowledge is valuable, but it should always be guided by God's Word.

2. True Education Restores God's Image

Sin damaged humanity, but God's plan of education helps restore His character in our lives through faith, learning, and obedience.

3. Education Develops the Whole Person

Seventh-day Adventist education aims to develop the mind, body, and spirit. Students are encouraged to grow academically, physically, morally, and spiritually.

4. Christ Is the Center

Jesus Christ is the foundation of Adventist education. Every subject should help students understand God's truth and prepare them for useful service.

5. Education Prepares Us for Eternity

The highest purpose of education is to prepare people not only for successful living now but also for eternal life with Jesus Christ.

Lesson Summary

  • God is the source of wisdom.
  • True education restores God's image.
  • Education develops the whole person.
  • Christ is the center of learning.
  • Education prepares us for service and eternity.
