🌿 Lesson 2: The Eight Laws of Health (NEWSTART)

Key Text

"Beloved, I wish above all things that thou mayest prosper and be in health, even as thy soul prospereth."

3 John 1:2

Lesson

The Seventh-day Adventist Health Message is often summarized by the word NEWSTART. These are eight principles that promote physical, mental, and spiritual well-being.

NEWSTART Principles

  • N – Nutrition: Eat wholesome foods God has provided.
  • E – Exercise: Keep the body active through regular physical activity.
  • W – Water: Drink sufficient clean water daily.
  • S – Sunshine: Receive moderate sunlight for health.
  • T – Temperance: Practice moderation and avoid harmful substances.
  • A – Air: Breathe fresh air whenever possible.
  • R – Rest: Get adequate sleep and Sabbath rest.
  • T – Trust in God: Depend upon God for strength and healing.

These principles have helped millions of people enjoy healthier and longer lives. They are based on Biblical teachings and supported by scientific research.
