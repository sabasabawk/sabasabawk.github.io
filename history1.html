The Protestant Reformation

Background

Before the rise of the Seventh-day Adventist Church, God worked through the Protestant Reformation to restore Bible truth.

For centuries many biblical teachings had been hidden by tradition. Reformers called people back to Scripture.

Important Reformers

  • John Wycliffe
  • Jan Hus
  • Martin Luther
  • Ulrich Zwingli
  • John Calvin

Martin Luther

In 1517 Martin Luther nailed his 95 Theses to the church door in Wittenberg, Germany. This event is often considered the beginning of the Protestant Reformation.

He taught that salvation comes through faith in Jesus Christ and that the Bible is the supreme authority for Christian belief.

Importance to SDA History

The Reformation restored many Bible truths and prepared the way for later movements including the Advent Movement that eventually led to the formation of the Seventh-day Adventist Church.
