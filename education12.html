🎓 Lesson 12: Lifelong Learning and Preparing for Eternity

Key Text

"And this is life eternal, that they might know thee the only true God, and Jesus Christ, whom thou hast sent."

John 17:3

Introduction

Education does not end with graduation. God calls His children to continue learning throughout life. True education prepares us not only for success on earth but also for eternal life with Jesus Christ.

Course Review

During this course you have learned about:

  • The meaning of Seventh-day Adventist education.
  • The biblical foundation of education.
  • God as the first Teacher.
  • The purpose of true education.
  • The home as the first school.
  • The role of Christian teachers.
  • Developing Christlike character.
  • Faith and learning together.
  • Health, work, and practical education.
  • The worldwide Adventist education system.
  • Preparing for service in this world and the next.

The Final Goal

The highest purpose of education is to restore the image of God in humanity and prepare people for Christ's everlasting kingdom. Knowledge is valuable, but a relationship with Jesus Christ is priceless.

"The fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom."

Proverbs 9:10

Final Challenge

Continue growing spiritually, intellectually, physically, and socially. Let everything you learn bring glory to God, strengthen your faith, and prepare you to serve others until Jesus returns.
