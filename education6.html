👩‍🏫 Lesson 6: The Role of Christian Teachers

Key Text

"Be ye followers of me, even as I also am of Christ."

1 Corinthians 11:1

Introduction

Teaching is one of the greatest responsibilities God gives to His people. Christian teachers do more than explain lessons—they help shape lives, build character, and lead students closer to Jesus Christ. Their influence often lasts for a lifetime.

1. A Teacher Is a Spiritual Leader

Christian teachers are called to guide students toward God's truth. Their classroom should be a place where faith, wisdom, and respect are encouraged.

2. Teach by Example

Students watch the lives of their teachers. Honesty, kindness, patience, humility, and fairness speak louder than words.

3. Love Every Student

Jesus showed love to everyone. Christian teachers should respect every learner, encourage those who struggle, and treat all students fairly without favoritism.

4. Encourage Excellence

Teachers should inspire students to work diligently and use their talents for the glory of God. Excellence honors the Creator who gave us our abilities.

5. Depend on God

Successful Christian teaching requires prayer and the guidance of the Holy Spirit. God gives wisdom, patience, and strength for every challenge.

Lesson Summary

  • Christian teachers are spiritual leaders.
  • Students learn through the teacher's example.
  • Every student deserves love and respect.
  • Teachers encourage excellence for God's glory.
  • Prayer and the Holy Spirit are essential in teaching.
