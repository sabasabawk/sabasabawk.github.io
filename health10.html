🚭 Lesson 10: Harmful Effects of Alcohol and Tobacco

Key Text

"Know ye not that ye are the temple of God, and that the Spirit of God dwelleth in you?"

1 Corinthians 3:16

Lesson

The Bible teaches that our bodies are temples of the Holy Spirit. Therefore, Christians are called to care for their health and avoid practices that harm the body and mind.

Alcohol impairs judgment, weakens self-control, damages relationships, and can contribute to serious health problems. Tobacco contains harmful chemicals that damage the lungs, heart, and many other organs.

The Seventh-day Adventist Church encourages complete abstinence from alcohol, tobacco, and other harmful substances because they interfere with physical, mental, and spiritual well-being.

God desires His people to enjoy abundant life through healthy choices. Through Christ's power, people can overcome harmful habits and live healthier lives.

Why Avoid Harmful Substances?

  • They damage the body.
  • They affect clear thinking and judgment.
  • They can lead to addiction.
  • They weaken spiritual growth.
  • They reduce quality of life.
