Fundamental Belief #14

Unity in the Body of Christ

Key Bible Texts

John 17:20-23

Ephesians 4:1-6

1 Corinthians 12:12-13

Colossians 3:14

What We Believe

The church is one body in Christ, composed of many members from every nation, tribe, language, and people. Although believers are diverse in culture, background, and gifts, they are united in Jesus Christ.

Unity in the church is based on Scripture, love, and the shared mission of proclaiming the Gospel. The Holy Spirit creates unity among believers and enables them to work together in harmony.

This unity does not remove diversity but celebrates it while maintaining oneness in faith, purpose, and doctrine.

Why It Matters

Unity reflects the character of Christ and strengthens the mission of the church. When believers are united, the world sees the love of God and the power of the Gospel.

Practical Application

  • Love and respect fellow believers.
  • Avoid division and gossip.
  • Work together in church mission.
  • Promote peace and forgiveness.
  • Celebrate diversity in the body of Christ.

Reflection Questions

1. Am I contributing to unity in my church?

2. How can I show more love to other believers?

3. What divides Christians today, and how can it be healed?
