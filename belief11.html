Fundamental Belief #11

Growing in Christ

Key Bible Texts

Colossians 2:6-7

Ephesians 6:10-18

2 Corinthians 3:18

John 17:17

What We Believe

By His death on the cross, Jesus triumphed over the forces of evil. The same Jesus who defeated evil spirits during His earthly ministry has broken their power and made certain their ultimate doom.

In Christ we are called to grow into His likeness in character, communion with God, and service to others. Through prayer, Bible study, worship, fellowship, and witnessing, believers mature spiritually and become more effective disciples.

As we surrender daily to the Holy Spirit, Christ lives in us, giving victory over fear, temptation, and sinful habits. Through His grace we continue growing until we reflect His character.

Why It Matters

The Christian life is not a one-time experience but a daily journey. Growing in Christ helps believers develop a stronger relationship with God, overcome spiritual challenges, and prepare for Christ's return.

Practical Application

  • Spend time with God every day in prayer.
  • Study and apply the Bible.
  • Participate in worship and fellowship.
  • Resist temptation through Christ's power.
  • Share your faith with others.

Reflection Questions

1. How am I growing spiritually?

2. What areas of my life need Christ's transforming power?

3. How can I strengthen my relationship with God?
