Fundamental Belief #3

The Father

Key Bible Texts

John 3:16

1 John 4:8

Matthew 6:9

What We Believe

God the Father is the Creator, Source, Sustainer, and Sovereign of all creation. He is just, holy, merciful, compassionate, and abundant in steadfast love. The Father works together with the Son and the Holy Spirit in the work of creation, redemption, and restoration.

Through Jesus Christ, God has revealed His character as a loving heavenly Father who desires a personal relationship with every person. His love was demonstrated most clearly in giving His Son for the salvation of humanity.

Why It Matters

Knowing God as Father gives believers confidence, peace, and hope. We can trust His wisdom, depend on His care, and approach Him in prayer knowing that He loves us and desires what is best for our lives.

Practical Application

  • Pray to God daily as your Heavenly Father.
  • Trust His guidance in every circumstance.
  • Show love and compassion to others.
  • Thank Him for His blessings and care.
  • Live as a faithful child of God.

Reflection Questions

1. What does it mean to call God my Father?

2. How have I experienced God's love in my life?

3. How can I trust Him more fully each day?
