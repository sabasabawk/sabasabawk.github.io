📖 Lesson 10: The Seven Seals of Revelation

Key Text

"Weep not: behold, the Lion of the tribe of Juda, the Root of David, hath prevailed to open the book, and to loose the seven seals thereof."

Revelation 5:5

Introduction

In Revelation chapters 5 and 6, John saw a scroll sealed with seven seals.

Only Jesus Christ was worthy to open the seals and reveal God's plans for His church throughout history.

The First Seal – White Horse

The first seal revealed a white horse and its rider.

The white color symbolizes purity and victory.

It represents the early Christian church spreading the gospel with power.

The Second Seal – Red Horse

The second seal revealed a red horse.

The color red symbolizes bloodshed and persecution.

This period represents the persecution of Christians by pagan Rome.

The Third Seal – Black Horse

The third seal revealed a black horse.

Black symbolizes spiritual darkness.

During this period, truth became mixed with error and many biblical teachings were neglected.

The Fourth Seal – Pale Horse

The fourth seal revealed a pale horse.

Its rider was called Death.

This represents a period of great spiritual decline and suffering.

The Fifth Seal – The Cry of the Martyrs

John saw faithful believers symbolically crying out for justice.

This seal reminds us that God remembers those who suffered for their faith.

A day of judgment and justice is coming.

The Sixth Seal – Signs in Heaven and Earth

The sixth seal describes great signs including:

  • A great earthquake
  • The darkening of the sun
  • The moon appearing as blood
  • The falling of the stars

These signs point toward the nearness of Christ's return.

The Seventh Seal – Silence in Heaven

When the seventh seal was opened, there was silence in heaven for a short period.

Many understand this to symbolize heaven's attention focused on the Second Coming of Christ.

The Central Message

The seven seals reveal that Christ has guided His church through every age.

Even during persecution and spiritual darkness, God remained faithful to His people.

Lessons for Today

  • Jesus controls the course of history.
  • God never forgets His faithful people.
  • Prophecy strengthens faith.
  • Christ's return is drawing near.

Application

Trust Jesus completely. The same Savior who guided His church through history is guiding His people today and preparing them for His glorious return.
