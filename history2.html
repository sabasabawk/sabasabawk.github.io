📜 History 2: William Miller and the Millerite Movement

Introduction

William Miller was an American farmer, military veteran, and Bible student who became one of the most influential religious figures of the nineteenth century. His careful study of Bible prophecy led him to believe that Jesus Christ would return very soon.

Studying Bible Prophecy

Miller spent years studying the Bible, especially the prophecies found in the books of Daniel and Revelation. He used the principle that one prophetic day represents one literal year and focused on the prophecy of 2,300 days in Daniel 8:14.

The Millerite Movement

Beginning in the 1830s, William Miller preached throughout the United States. Thousands of people accepted his message and eagerly anticipated the Second Coming of Christ. These believers became known as Millerites.

A Worldwide Awakening

The Advent message spread rapidly through preaching, books, newspapers, and public meetings. Many people turned to God, studied the Bible, and prepared their hearts for Christ's return.

Preparing the Way

Although the Millerites misunderstood the event that would occur at the end of the 2,300-year prophecy, their movement prepared the way for the birth of the Seventh-day Adventist Church after 1844.
