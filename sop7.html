🏡 Lesson 7: Counsel on Christian Living

Key Text

"Whether therefore ye eat, or drink, or whatsoever ye do, do all to the glory of God."

1 Corinthians 10:31

Lesson Study

The Spirit of Prophecy provides practical counsel for daily Christian living. Ellen G. White emphasized that Christianity is not only a belief system but a way of life that reflects the character of Jesus.

Her writings encourage believers to develop habits of prayer, Bible study, honesty, purity, kindness, humility, and service. These qualities help Christians grow spiritually and become effective witnesses for Christ.

She taught that every area of life should be surrendered to God, including our thoughts, words, actions, relationships, and use of time.

Christian living involves making choices that honor God and avoiding influences that weaken faith. Through the power of the Holy Spirit, believers can experience victory over sin and grow in Christlike character.

The goal of Christian living is to reveal the love of Jesus to the world and prepare for His soon return.

Principles of Christian Living

  • Pray daily.
  • Study the Bible regularly.
  • Practice honesty and integrity.
  • Show kindness and compassion.
  • Use time wisely.
  • Serve others unselfishly.
  • Live for the glory of God.

Memory Verse
