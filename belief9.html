Fundamental Belief #9

The Life, Death, and Resurrection of Christ

Key Bible Texts

John 3:16

Romans 5:8

1 Corinthians 15:3-4

1 Peter 2:21-22

What We Believe

In Christ's life of perfect obedience to God's will, His suffering, death, and resurrection, God provided the only means of atonement for human sin. Jesus lived a sinless life, revealing the character of God and demonstrating how humanity should live.

On the cross, Christ died in our place. He bore the penalty for sin, making reconciliation between God and humanity possible. His sacrifice was complete, sufficient, and available to everyone who believes.

Through His resurrection, Jesus conquered death and assured believers of eternal life. His victory gives hope, forgiveness, and confidence in God's promise of salvation.

Why It Matters

Without Christ's death and resurrection there would be no salvation. His sacrifice demonstrates God's love and provides forgiveness, while His resurrection guarantees victory over sin and death.

Practical Application

  • Accept Christ's sacrifice by faith.
  • Live a life of gratitude and obedience.
  • Follow Jesus' example of love and service.
  • Share the Gospel with others.
  • Find hope in Christ during difficult times.

Reflection Questions

1. What does Christ's sacrifice mean to me personally?

2. How does His resurrection strengthen my faith?

3. How can I reflect Christ's love in my daily life?
