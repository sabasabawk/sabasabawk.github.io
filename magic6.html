⭐ Lesson 6: Astrology and Divination

Key Text

"Should not a people seek unto their God? for the living to the dead?"

Isaiah 8:19

Introduction

Many people read horoscopes, consult astrologers, or seek information about the future through divination. These practices have existed for thousands of years and remain popular today.

The Bible teaches that God's people should seek wisdom and guidance from God rather than from astrology or fortune telling.

What Is Astrology?

Astrology is the belief that the positions of stars, planets, and other heavenly bodies influence human lives and events.

Astrologers often create horoscopes and predictions based on birth dates and star signs.

What Is Divination?

Divination is any attempt to discover hidden knowledge or predict the future through supernatural means apart from God.

Examples include fortune telling, tarot cards, crystal balls, palm reading, and similar practices.

The Bible's Warning

God repeatedly warned His people not to practice divination or seek guidance from those who claimed secret knowledge.

Instead, He called His people to trust Him and obey His Word.

Daniel and the Wise Men

In the book of Daniel, the astrologers and wise men of Babylon could not reveal King Nebuchadnezzar's dream.

God revealed the dream to Daniel because Daniel sought wisdom from God through prayer.

Why Astrology Cannot Replace God

  • Only God knows the future perfectly.
  • God desires a personal relationship with His people.
  • Astrology can lead people away from faith in God.
  • The Bible provides trustworthy guidance.
  • Prayer is more powerful than horoscopes.

Trusting God's Guidance

Christians should not base important decisions on star signs or predictions.

Instead, we should seek God's wisdom through prayer, Bible study, and godly counsel.

Jesus Is Our Guide

Jesus promised to lead His followers through the Holy Spirit.

When we trust Him, we do not need secret knowledge about the future because we know the One who holds the future.
