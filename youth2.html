👥 Youth Lesson 2: Identity in Christ

Key Text

"Therefore if any man be in Christ, he is a new creature: old things are passed away; behold, all things are become new."

2 Corinthians 5:17

Introduction

Many young people struggle with questions such as: Who am I? Why am I here? What is my purpose?

The world often tells us that our value comes from appearance, popularity, talents, or possessions. God teaches something different. Our true identity is found in Jesus Christ.

Created by God

Every person is created by God with value and purpose. You are not an accident. God knew you before you were born and has a plan for your life.

Because you are God's creation, your worth does not depend on what others think about you.

Children of God

When we accept Jesus Christ as our Savior, we become children of God. This is the greatest identity a person can have.

As God's children, we are loved, forgiven, and accepted by Him.

A New Creation

Christ changes lives. When we follow Him, He transforms our hearts, attitudes, and actions.

Old habits and sinful ways no longer define who we are.

Rejecting False Identities

The world may try to define you by:

  • Your appearance.
  • Your social status.
  • Your mistakes.
  • Your failures.
  • The opinions of others.

God defines you by His love and grace.

Living with Purpose

God has given every young person gifts, talents, and opportunities to serve Him.

Understanding your identity in Christ helps you live with confidence, courage, and purpose.

Growing in Christ

  • Pray daily.
  • Study the Bible.
  • Choose godly friends.
  • Serve others.
  • Trust God's plan for your life.
