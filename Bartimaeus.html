BLIND BARTIMAEUS

When Jesus Stops For You — Mark 10:46–52

“Then they came to Jericho. As Jesus and His disciples, together with a large crowd, were leaving the city, a blind man, Bartimaeus… was sitting by the roadside begging.” — Mark 10:46–52

Introduction

Life can sometimes make people feel like Bartimaeus — rejected, forgotten, limited, dependent on others, and sitting beside the road while others move forward. Bartimaeus was blind physically, but many people today are blind spiritually, emotionally, mentally, or financially.

But one day Jesus passed by Bartimaeus, and everything changed. This sermon teaches us that when Jesus passes by, no condition is permanent.

1. Bartimaeus Knew His Condition

“A blind beggar named Bartimaeus…”

Bartimaeus did not pretend to be fine. Healing begins when a person recognizes their need.

  • Some are spiritually blind
  • Some are emotionally wounded
  • Some are trapped in sin
  • Some have lost purpose

Lesson: God cannot heal what you continue to hide.

2. Bartimaeus Heard That Jesus Was Passing By

“When he heard that it was Jesus of Nazareth…”

Bartimaeus could not see Jesus, but he could hear about Him. Faith often begins through hearing.

Lesson: Do not ignore the moment when Jesus is passing through your life.

3. Bartimaeus Cried Out To Jesus

“Jesus, Son of David, have mercy on me!”

Bartimaeus prayed with desperation. Some blessings come only to those who refuse to remain silent.

Lesson: A desperate cry can bring divine attention.

4. The Crowd Tried To Silence Him

“Many rebuked him and told him to be quiet…”

Whenever you are close to breakthrough, opposition appears. But Bartimaeus refused to stop.

Lesson: Never allow people to silence your faith.

5. Jesus Stood Still

“So Jesus stood still…”

Jesus was surrounded by crowds, yet one desperate cry made Him stop.

Lesson: Jesus still stops for those who call upon Him sincerely.

6. Bartimaeus Threw Away His Garment

“Throwing aside his garment, he rose and came to Jesus.”

The garment represented his old life. To move forward, some things must be left behind.

  • Fear
  • Doubt
  • Bitterness
  • Wrong relationships

Lesson: You cannot fully embrace your future while holding onto your past.

7. Jesus Asked Him A Question

“What do you want Me to do for you?”

Jesus wanted Bartimaeus to express his desire. God wants us to pray specifically.

Lesson: Be specific in prayer.

8. Faith Brought His Miracle

“Go your way; your faith has made you well.”

Faith made him cry out, ignore the crowd, and receive healing.

Lesson: Without faith it is impossible to please God.

9. Bartimaeus Followed Jesus

“Immediately he received his sight and followed Jesus on the road.”

His story did not end with healing. He became a follower of Christ.

Lesson: After God blesses you, continue walking with Him.

Key Lessons From Bartimaeus

  • Admit your need
  • Listen for God’s voice
  • Cry out to Jesus
  • Ignore discouragement
  • Believe Jesus hears you
  • Leave behind old things
  • Pray specifically
  • Hold onto faith
  • Follow Jesus after the miracle

Conclusion

Bartimaeus began the day blind, begging, and forgotten. But after encountering Jesus:

  • His eyes opened
  • His life changed
  • His future changed
  • His direction changed

One encounter with Jesus can change everything. Jesus still stops for desperate people.
