Fundamental Belief #24

Christ's Ministry in the Heavenly Sanctuary

Key Bible Texts

Hebrews 8:1-2

Hebrews 4:14-16

Hebrews 9:11-12

Daniel 8:14

1 John 2:1

What We Believe

There is a sanctuary in heaven, the true tabernacle established by God and not by humans. In it, Christ ministers on our behalf as our great High Priest.

After His ascension, Jesus began His ministry in the heavenly sanctuary, applying the benefits of His sacrificial death for all who accept Him by faith. He intercedes for believers, providing forgiveness, grace, and strength.

The earthly sanctuary in the Old Testament pointed forward to Christ's work in the heavenly sanctuary. Through His ministry, God's plan of salvation is being carried forward until its final completion.

Jesus Our High Priest

  • Represents believers before the Father.
  • Intercedes on behalf of His people.
  • Provides mercy and grace.
  • Applies the benefits of His sacrifice.
  • Prepares God's people for His return.

Why It Matters

Because Jesus ministers in heaven for us, we can confidently approach God through prayer. We have assurance that Christ understands our struggles, forgives our sins, and helps us live victorious Christian lives.

Practical Application

  • Trust Jesus as your High Priest.
  • Pray with confidence.
  • Seek forgiveness through Christ.
  • Live each day in faith and obedience.
  • Prepare for Christ's soon return.

Reflection Questions

1. What does it mean that Jesus intercedes for me?

2. How does Christ's ministry strengthen my faith?

3. Am I daily relying on Jesus for grace and guidance?
