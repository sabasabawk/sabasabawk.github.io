💧 Lesson 4: Water and Its Importance

Key Text

"Jesus answered and said unto her, Whosoever drinketh of this water shall thirst again: But whosoever drinketh of the water that I shall give him shall never thirst."

John 4:13-14

Lesson

Water is one of God's greatest gifts for health and life. The human body is made up largely of water, and every organ depends on it to function properly.

Water helps regulate body temperature, transport nutrients, remove waste products, lubricate joints, and maintain healthy digestion.

Many health problems can result from insufficient water intake, including fatigue, headaches, poor concentration, and dehydration.

The Adventist Health Message encourages drinking clean water regularly and using water wisely for hygiene and health.

Just as water is essential for physical life, Jesus Christ is the Living Water who satisfies our spiritual thirst.

Benefits of Water

  • Maintains body hydration.
  • Supports healthy digestion.
  • Helps regulate body temperature.
  • Removes toxins from the body.
  • Improves physical and mental performance.
