💰 Lesson 5: Saving and Preparing for the Future

Key Text

"Go to the ant, thou sluggard; consider her ways, and be wise."

Proverbs 6:6

Introduction

The Bible teaches that wise people prepare for the future. Saving money is not a sign of lacking faith in God. Instead, it is an act of wisdom and responsible stewardship.

Learning from the Ant

In Proverbs 6:6–8, the ant gathers food during harvest to prepare for future needs. God uses this small creature to teach us the importance of diligence, planning, and saving.

Why Should Christians Save?

  • To prepare for emergencies.
  • To support our families.
  • To avoid unnecessary debt.
  • To be able to help others in times of need.
  • To remain faithful stewards of God's blessings.

Saving Does Not Mean Hoarding

The Bible warns against storing wealth selfishly while ignoring God's work or the needs of others. Christians should save wisely while remaining generous and trusting in God.

Joseph's Example

In Genesis 41, God gave Joseph wisdom to store grain during seven years of plenty. Because of careful planning, Egypt survived seven years of famine.

Joseph's example teaches us that preparing during good times enables us to endure difficult seasons.

Practical Ways to Save

  • Spend less than you earn.
  • Set aside money regularly.
  • Avoid unnecessary purchases.
  • Prepare for unexpected expenses.
  • Continue returning faithful tithes and offerings.

Application

Ask God to give you wisdom in managing your finances. Begin setting aside a portion of your income each month while trusting Him as your Provider.
