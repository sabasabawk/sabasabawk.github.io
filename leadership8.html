🤝 Lesson 8: Handling Conflict Biblically

Key Text

"Blessed are the peacemakers: for they shall be called the children of God."

Matthew 5:9

Introduction

Conflict is a part of life. Even faithful Christians sometimes disagree. A godly leader does not ignore conflict or make it worse but seeks peace through biblical principles.

1. Seek Peace First

God calls His people to be peacemakers. Christian leaders should always seek reconciliation rather than division.

2. Speak the Truth in Love

When correction is necessary, it should be given with kindness, respect, and love. Harsh words often create greater conflict.

3. Listen Before Speaking

Wise leaders carefully listen to every side before making decisions. Understanding comes before judgment.

"Wherefore, my beloved brethren, let every man be swift to hear, slow to speak, slow to wrath."

James 1:19

4. Be Willing to Forgive

Forgiveness reflects the heart of Christ. Leaders who forgive help restore broken relationships and strengthen unity.

5. Pray for Wisdom

Every conflict should be taken to God in prayer. The Holy Spirit gives wisdom to resolve disagreements in a Christ-like manner.

Lesson Summary

  • God calls leaders to be peacemakers.
  • Truth should always be spoken with love.
  • Listening is an important leadership skill.
  • Forgiveness restores relationships.
  • Prayer brings God's wisdom into every situation.
